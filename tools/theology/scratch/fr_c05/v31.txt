ch31.t | The Spirit of Truth
ch31.sub | The New Teacher Who Leads into All Truth
ch31.sum | Before he departed, Jesus promised his followers that he would send another teacher, and at Pentecost the Spirit of Truth was poured out upon all flesh. This spirit is not the letter or law of truth but the conviction of living and growing truth, and it makes us conscious not of itself but of Michael the Son. The Spirit of Truth was given not only to the apostles but to all sincere believers, and from then on Thought Adjusters came to indwell every normal mind. This chapter considers in turn the promise of the new teacher, the nature of the Spirit of Truth, the meaning of Pentecost, and the mission and fruits of that spirit. If religion before Pentecost showed man seeking God, after Pentecost it also shows God seeking man. The Spirit of Truth makes the Jesus of history the living experience of believers today.
ch31.s1 | The Promised New Teacher
ch31.s1.u1.k | Jesus promised to send another teacher in his stead, the Spirit of Truth, after ascending to the Father.
ch31.s1.u1.p | The promise of the Spirit of Truth was there from early in Jesus' public life. He said that just as the Father's spirit indwells human minds, so after his work was done the Spirit of Truth would be poured out on all flesh, and that together these spirits would establish believers in the kingdom of spiritual understanding. After ascending to the Father, he kept that promise at Pentecost. His departure was not abandonment but a way of coming nearer.
ch31.s1.u2.k | As Jesus had lived with his disciples, he would now live in them and lead them into all truth.
ch31.s1.u2.p | At the Last Supper Jesus promised his disciples, on the eve of parting, a new relationship. Until then he had been beside them; now he would be within them, one with their personal experience. As the Father had given him his spirit, so he would give them his. That spirit would guide and comfort and at last lead them into all truth. Presence beside them would become presence within them.
ch31.s1.u3.k | The Spirit of Truth is poured out on all flesh, but becomes a well of living water only in those who receive him.
ch31.s1.u3.p | The gift is given to all, but receiving it is each person's choice. His spirit is poured out upon all flesh, but not everyone will receive it as the guide and counselor of the soul. Yet those who accept him as counselor of the soul are enlightened, cleansed, and comforted. The spirit becomes not a guest who calls now and then from outside but a spring welling up unceasingly within.
ch31.s2 | The Nature of the Spirit of Truth
ch31.s2.u1.k | The Spirit of Truth is not the letter or law of truth but the conviction of living and growing truth.
ch31.s2.u1.p | To think of the Spirit of Truth as a guarantee for doctrine is to miss its nature. This spirit is not a form of truth but the conviction of true meanings, the spirit of truth that is ever unfolding, adapting, and growing. So truth can be known, lived, and experienced, but never imprisoned in formulas or creeds. The moment truth is locked in a human framework it becomes dead theory. Living truth breathes only in experience.
ch31.s2.u2.k | The Spirit of Truth creates consciousness not of himself but of Michael the Son.
ch31.s2.u2.p | The proof of experiencing the Spirit of Truth is not an intense awareness of the spirit. This spirit does not display himself; he only deepens fellowship with Jesus. So if we would know whether we commune with the Spirit of Truth, we should ask not about mystical feelings but whether our relationship with Christ has grown. If the Adjuster is the presence of the Father, the Spirit of Truth is the presence of the Sons.
ch31.s2.u3.k | The working of the Spirit of Truth depends on how fully man receives it.
ch31.s2.u3.p | The Spirit of Truth has been poured out on everyone, but its power depends on each person's capacity of receptivity. Prayer does not bring the spirit, yet it digs larger and deeper channels through which the divine bestowals may flow. Within a person the Father's Adjuster, the Son's Spirit of Truth, and the Universe Spirit's Holy Spirit work together, and these three gifts are unified as the ministry of the Supreme. God dwells in man through a threefold gift.
ch31.s3 | Pentecost
ch31.s3.u1.k | At Pentecost the praying believers became aware of a new presence and of spiritual joy and confidence.
ch31.s3.u1.p | The event of Pentecost began not with dramatic signs but with inner change. The one hundred and twenty believers sensed a strange presence in the room, and at once became conscious of deep joy, security, and confidence. Peter recognized it as the Spirit of Truth the Master had promised. The coming of the spirit turned those who had hidden in fear into witnesses.
ch31.s3.u2.k | The Spirit of Truth was given to all sincere believers, and now God is seen seeking man.
ch31.s3.u2.p | The gift of Pentecost was not a privilege of the apostles alone. Every man and woman in the upper room received the spirit, as did honest hearts throughout the world. So true religion was freed from the custody of priests and sacred classes and came to find expression in individual souls. More remarkable still is the change of direction: to a religion of man seeking God was added God seeking man and dwelling within him.
ch31.s3.u3.k | After the Spirit of Truth was poured out, Thought Adjusters came to indwell all normal will creatures.
ch31.s3.u3.p | The bestowal of the Spirit of Truth also opened the way for another divine gift. When a bestowal Son's mission is completed and the Spirit of Truth is poured out on all, Adjusters flock to that world to indwell every normal mind. Then the Adjuster truly becomes the kingdom of heaven within you. The Father's spirit now dwells not in a favored few but in every normal mind.
ch31.s4 | The Mission and Fruits of the Spirit of Truth
ch31.s4.u1.k | The Spirit of Truth fosters truth, removes the believer's loneliness, and makes Jesus' life live anew.
ch31.s4.u1.p | The mission of the Spirit of Truth has several strands. First it fosters truth, for understanding truth is the highest liberty a person can know. Next it removes the orphaned loneliness believers would feel after Jesus' departure. It also helps them recall his words, reinterpret his life, and live afresh in each generation of believers the life he lived in the flesh.
ch31.s4.u2.k | The Spirit of Truth purifies the heart and is a force leading man upward and urging him onward.
ch31.s4.u2.p | The charge that religion is an opiate that numbs people does not fit the religion of Jesus. On the cross he refused even the drug that would have dulled his pain, and his spirit does not lull people to sleep but raises them up. The Spirit of Truth turns selfishness into selflessness and gathers life's purpose around God's will and the welfare of others. Its joy strengthens body, mind, and soul alike.
ch31.s4.u3.k | In the Spirit of Truth, the golden rule becomes a call to love others as Jesus loved us.
ch31.s4.u3.p | The same golden rule means different things depending on the spirit in which it is read. Read only by the letter it is a rule of conduct; in the Spirit of Truth it becomes a living reality. When those led by the Spirit of Truth grasp its true meaning, they overflow with the assurance of citizenship in a friendly universe, and find satisfaction only in loving others as Jesus loved us.
ch31.qs1 | If I measure fellowship with the Spirit of Truth not by mystical feelings but by a deepened relationship with Jesus, where does my faith stand now?
ch31.qs2 | Are there places where I am imprisoning truth in formulas or creeds and turning it into dead truth?
ch31.qs3 | What comfort and what responsibility does the Pentecostal message of a God who seeks man bring me today?

La vraie signification cosmique de cette règle de relations universelles ne se révèle que dans sa réalisation spirituelle, dans l’interprétation de la loi de conduite par l’esprit du Fils envers l’esprit du Père qui habite l’âme du mortel. Quand les mortels ainsi dirigés par l’esprit réalisent la véritable signification de cette règle d’or, ils débordent de l’assurance qu’ils sont citoyens d’un univers amical, et leurs idéaux de réalité d’esprit ne sont satisfaits que s’ils aiment leurs semblables comme Jésus nous a tous aimés. Telle est la réalité de la réalisation de l’amour de Dieu.
