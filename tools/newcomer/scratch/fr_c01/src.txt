Each week runs to about twenty pages, with quotations from The Urantia Book and commentary in roughly equal measure. Hearing the voice of the text itself matters most, but a first-time reader also needs a patient guide to help that voice be understood. Wherever words alone would make a point hard to follow, a chart or diagram tries to show it instead."
