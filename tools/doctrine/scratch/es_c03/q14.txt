ch14:0 "In the previous chapter mankind began with the decision of a single pair, Andon and Fonta. Yet the humanity of today is divided into many skin colors and many body types. Where did this diversity come from? The Urantia Book does not explain the origin of the races as an accident or a curse. The differentiation of the races is part of a plan for which the Life Carriers made provision from the beginning, and that plan extends to producing many races, scattering them, and then gathering them into one again. Following the text, this chapter asks in turn why the races arose, how they arose, where they were dispersed, and toward what they are moving."
ch14:1 "1. The Differentiation of the Races Is Part of the Plan"
ch14:2 "The need for racial differentiation first shows itself in the tendencies of evolving creatures. As the Andonic clans multiplied, contact between families gave rise to friction and feuds, and wars between the clans took away the most capable strains (63:4.7–4.8). The revelator tells us that the Life Carriers knew of this tendency beforehand."
ch14:4 "On an ordinary evolutionary world these six races appear one after another in the order of the colors of the spectrum, beginning with the red man (52:1.1): the red man, the orange race, the yellow man, the green race, the blue man, and the indigo race. Of these the revelator calls the first, third, and fifth, the red, the yellow, and the blue, the primary races, and the other three the secondary races (51:4.3). But this differentiation is not merely a loss."
ch14:6 "Differentiation is not an end in itself but a preparation for a greater synthesis. Because no single race can contain every potential, many races express differing potentials, and when these are later brought together again, mankind is enriched. This logic guides the whole chapter."

ch14:52 "In Closing: From One Household to One Blood"
ch14:53 "The doctrine of the races traces a circle. The Life Carriers provided for the separation of the races so that man, the descendant of fighting animals, could live together. The six colored races of Urantia were born as brothers and sisters in a single family, the Sangik family, and scattered over the world, each with its own characteristics and achievements. Those differences served natural selection, competition, and tolerance; they did not divide standing before God. The next chapter examines the first of them, the Lucifer rebellion and the betrayal of Caligastia."
