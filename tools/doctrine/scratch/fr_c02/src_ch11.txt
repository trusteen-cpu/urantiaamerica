### ch11:label
Chapter 11
### ch11:title
The Thought Adjuster
### ch11:sub
God Indwelling
### ch11:0
In the previous two chapters man received two gifts. Personality comes directly from the Universal Father, and mind comes from the Infinite Spirit through a series of ministers. Yet the most astonishing reality in man, as The Urantia Book describes him, is a third gift: God causes a part of himself to dwell directly in the human mind. Paper 107 opens with this fact.
### ch11:2
This paradox, that the One who is farthest away is also nearest, is the starting point of the doctrine of the Adjuster. This chapter asks five questions. What is the Adjuster? Why does God come into man? When, and under what conditions, does he come? What does he do in the mind? And where does the relationship between man and the Adjuster end?
### ch11:3
1. Definition: A Fragment of God
### ch11:4
Concerning the origin of the Adjusters there is no difference of opinion in the universe: they proceed directly from the Universal Father. The revelator presses the meaning still further.
### ch11:6
The Adjuster is not a creature made by God but a fragment of God himself. That is why the Adjuster is of a different order from the angels or other spirit ministers. At the same time, the Adjuster is not a personality. Paper 107 says, “Thought Adjusters are not personalities, but they are real entities” (107:7.1), and calls them prepersonal realities. Personality belongs to the man; the Adjuster belongs to the God who dwells within that man. This difference arises, as we saw in Chapter 9, from the fact that personality and the Adjuster are different gifts. The Adjuster is not a cold principle but a presence of love. The introduction to Paper 107 portrays the Adjusters in these words.
### ch11:8
The word ‘imprisoned’ shows the humility of this indwelling. A fragment of the infinite God consents to abide in the narrow place of a finite mortal mind. And that abiding is itself a promise of man's eternal career. The various names by which the Adjuster is called illuminate various aspects of his nature.

### ch11:10
2. The Ground: Why God Comes into Man
### ch11:11
The reason God indwells man lies in the goal God has set for man. That goal is one man could never reach by his own power.
### ch11:13
The logic is simple. First comes the command to be perfect, and then the Adjuster comes as the way to fulfill that command. God does not merely set the goal and withdraw; he himself becomes a partner on the road to that goal. The same section therefore calls the Adjuster's indwelling “the absolute and unqualified assurance” (107:0.3) that man can find the Father. The very fact of an indwelling God is the guarantee that God can be found.
### ch11:15
The fatherhood of God does not end as a confession or a metaphor. Because a fragment of the Father actually dwells in man, man is actually a son of God. The doctrine of the fatherhood of God becomes fact in the indwelling of the Adjuster.
### ch11:16
3. The Indwelling: When and Where He Comes
### ch11:17
The Adjuster does not come at just any time. Paper 108, section 2, “Prerequisites of Adjuster Indwelling,” says that the mind must first be prepared.
### ch11:19
Here the ministry of the seven adjutant mind-spirits, seen in Chapter 10, bears fruit. When the spirit of worship and the spirit of wisdom are functioning and the child makes his first moral choice, the Adjuster comes. On Urantia this occurs, on the average, just before the sixth birthday (108:2.1). And since the bestowal of Michael and the pouring out of the Spirit of Truth, Adjusters indwell the minds of all normal will creatures (108:6.2).
### ch11:20
This indwelling does not wait until man has been made clean. The same section says that through the divine gifts the Father makes his closest approach to sin and evil, and adds, “for it is literally true that the Adjuster must coexist in the mortal mind even in the very midst of human unrighteousness” (108:6.2). The Adjuster suffers from man's selfish thoughts and animal fears and yet does not depart. The text assures us: “The Adjuster remains with you in all disaster and through every sickness which does not wholly destroy the mentality” (110:1.5). So persistent is the way God stays at man's side.

### ch11:22
The place where the Adjuster abides must also be made clear. The Adjuster is not located in any organ of the body.
### ch11:24
That the Adjuster indwells the mind dovetails with the conclusion of Chapter 10, that mind is the arena where the Adjuster and man meet. The Adjuster works upon the mind, and man chooses within the mind.
### ch11:25
4. The Mission: What He Does in the Mind
### ch11:26
The key to understanding the Adjuster's mission lies in the word ‘adjust.’ The Adjuster does not do man's thinking for him.
### ch11:28
Thinking is the part of personality. The Adjuster improves, modifies, and co-ordinates that thinking, and above all builds up its spiritual counterpart. The same section says that the Adjusters “are devoted to the work of building up spiritual counterparts of your careers, morontia transcripts of your true advancing selves” (110:2.3). Whatever of spiritual value is found in the decisions man makes on earth is reproduced in the Adjuster and preserved for the next life. Even truths man cannot yet receive are not discarded.
### ch11:30
The Adjuster does not lose what could not be accomplished in this life but carries it over into the next. For the Adjuster, life on earth is not an end but a stage. The Adjuster's mission, therefore, is not to make this life comfortable.
### ch11:32
Within man the Adjuster becomes a stimulus of a different kind from outward stimuli. The senses of the body reach the mind along the nerves, but the Adjuster's stimulus comes from within and from above.
### ch11:34
That life is not easy may be no proof that the Adjuster is absent; it may be a sign that the Adjuster is at work. For without decisions there is no growth, and without growth the soul does not grow. Yet even the Adjuster cannot make man's decisions for him. The Adjusters would like to change fear into love and confidence, “but they cannot mechanically and arbitrarily do such things; that is your task” (108:5.8).

### ch11:36
5. Co-operation: The Human Will and the Adjuster
### ch11:37
If the Adjuster is a fragment of God, why does the Adjuster's will bow to the will of man? The answer of The Urantia Book lies in the sovereignty of the will that the Father has decreed (5:6.8). The Adjuster does not overstep that sovereignty.
### ch11:39
But if the Adjuster also has volition, why is he subservient to the will of man? Paper 107 explains this by the difference in the levels on which will operates.
### ch11:41
Personality is the gift of the Father, and the Adjuster is a prepersonal reality. Therefore even a fragment of God respects the choice of personality. When the human mind fully conforms to the mind of the Adjuster and the will carries out the decisions of that combined mind, only then does the Adjuster's prepersonal will attain personality expression through man's decision (110:2.5). The will of God appears in human life only through the will of man. The relationship with the Adjuster is therefore one of co-operation. That co-operation is not necessarily a conscious process. The revelator says that man's motives and decisions, his faithful determinations and supreme desires, do constitute real co-operation (110:3.6). And he sets forth four ways of consciously augmenting that co-operation.

### ch11:43
In this co-operation it is not the Adjuster who fails: “Adjusters never fail” (110:3.3). Angels and even the Sons of the local universe may sometimes depart from the divine way, “but Adjusters never falter” (108:5.3). Success or failure is spoken of only from the standpoint of human survival. What, then, is the heart of this co-operation?
### ch11:45
The progress of co-operation shows itself in the conquest of the seven psychic circles. Entrance upon the seventh circle marks the beginning of true human personality function, and completion of the first circle denotes the relative maturity of the mortal being (110:6.1). As man passes through the circles one by one, attunement with the Adjuster deepens, the soul grows, and the personality becomes more real (110:6.8–110:6.10). As the circles are traversed, the voice of the Adjuster also draws nearer. That voice is always within man, yet most people “will hear it seldom during a lifetime” (110:7.9). Not to hear him does not mean that he is not there. Most of the Adjuster's work is done quietly, below the threshold of consciousness.
### ch11:46
6. Consequence: Fusion and Unending Revelation
### ch11:47
The end of co-operation is fusion. But the attainment of the circles is not the same as fusion. Fusion depends “upon the attainment of a final and complete attunement of the mortal will with the will of God as it is resident in the Thought Adjuster” (110:7.1). When that attunement is achieved, the two are no longer two.
### ch11:49
Note here that the partner in fusion is ‘the immortal morontia soul.’ The Adjuster does not fuse with man's material mind; he fuses with the soul he has nurtured together with man. The doctrine of the Adjuster thus leads into the doctrine of the soul. And fusion is not an end but the beginning of unending discovery.

### ch11:52
In Closing: God Who Is Nearest
### ch11:53
The doctrine of the Adjuster unites the transcendence and the immanence of God in a single reality. The Father at the center of Paradise sends a fragment of himself into the human mind. That fragment is not created; it is God himself, and it comes as a partner for fulfilling the command to be perfect. The Adjuster indwells the mind prepared by the adjutant mind-spirits at the time of the first moral decision; he spiritualizes thought rather than replacing it, and he stimulates decisions rather than making life easy. Because he never acts against the will of man, everything is accomplished through co-operation, and when that co-operation is complete, man and the Adjuster become one. Yet the partner in that fusion is the soul that man and the Adjuster bring forth together. The next chapter examines how this soul is born, how it grows, and where it goes.
