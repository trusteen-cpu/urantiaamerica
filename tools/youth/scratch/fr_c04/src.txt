p37.001 | Introduction
p37.002 | At the very top of all the personalities of Nebadon is the Creator Son, Michael, and beside him, as his equal partner, is the Divine Minister, the local universe Mother Spirit. These two are the father and mother of every being born in Nebadon. This paper mainly introduces a middle group among them called the Universe Aids, and it also takes a short look at the higher spirits who stay in Nebadon and at the permanent citizens of the local universe.
p37.003 | 1. The Universe Aids
p37.004 | There are seven orders of Universe Aids: the Bright and Morning Star, the Brilliant Evening Stars, the archangels, the Most High Assistants, the High Commissioners, the Celestial Overseers, and the Mansion World Teachers. The first, the Bright and Morning Star, is the firstborn of a local universe, and there is only one. In Nebadon he is Gabriel of Salvington. At first Gabriel worked almost alone, but as the universe grew, staff members and Evening Stars came into being to help him.
p37.005 | 2. The Brilliant Evening Stars
p37.006 | The Brilliant Evening Stars are beings designed by the Melchizedeks and created by the Creator Son and the Creative Spirit, and they serve mainly as Gabriel’s liaison officers. Since Gabriel cannot be in two places at once, the Evening Stars stand in for him at many gatherings and on many worlds. When they come to a planet on a special mission, they have sometimes been called “⟦Q 37:2.3⟧the angel of the Lord.⟦/Q⟧”
p37.007 | The Evening Stars are a unique order made up of those who were created that way from the beginning and those who rose to that position after serving below, the way seraphim do. There are 13,641 of them in Nebadon, and 8,809 of them are ascended ones. They always work in pairs, one created and one ascended. When an Avonal Son goes to a planet on a bestowal, two Evening Stars go along with him and direct the mission.
p37.008 | 3. The Archangels
p37.009 | The archangels are children of the Creator Son and the Universe Mother Spirit, and there are nearly eight hundred thousand of them in Nebadon. Instead of the everyday administration of the universe, they devote themselves to helping human beings survive death and ascend. Amazingly, a divisional headquarters of the archangels was recently set up on the small planet Urantia. When students from other universes see this and learn that Michael came to this world, they become very interested in our planet.
p37.010 | This tells us that more and more ascension work will gather on Michael’s bestowal world in the future, and it adds weight to the promise, “⟦Q 37:3.4⟧I will come again.⟦/Q⟧” The one who calls the sleepers to awaken when a Paradise Son ends an age is also the archangel of the resurrection. On the worlds of the archangels are the record keepers who write down and preserve the life of every human being.
p37.011 | 4. Most High Assistants
p37.012 | The Most High Assistants are beings who volunteer to come from outside the local universe, from the central universe and the superuniverse, and there are always several million of them. They help our universe grow to fit the ideals of Paradise better. They can be assigned to any kind of work, such as administration or teaching, and they are a great help to the rulers of the universe, especially in complicated decisions that require looking far ahead.
p37.013 | 5. High Commissioners
p37.014 | The High Commissioners are human beings who gained eternal life not by uniting with a Thought Adjuster, the spirit of the Father, but by uniting with a spirit of the Mother Spirit. On some worlds the Adjusters stay only for a while and then leave, so the people there take this path. Most of them do not go on to Paradise but stay and serve in the local universe.
p37.015 | First they work on the planets as racial commissioners, speaking for the views and needs of the different races and working to see that they are treated justly and fairly. As they gain experience, they become High Commissioners, and there are more than one and a half billion of them in Nebadon. In every court they explain a person’s circumstances and nature to the judges, and they speak for those who cannot speak for themselves. Their wisdom, which holds both the human and the divine point of view, is a great treasure for the universe.
p37.016 | 6. Celestial Overseers
p37.017 | The Celestial Overseers are more than three million educational advisers who travel to schools all over the universe to see that teaching is going well. The text says, “⟦Q 37:6.2⟧The entire universe is one vast school.⟦/Q⟧” At the heart of this education is building character through experience, living things through for yourself while being given insight.
p37.018 | Education in Nebadon works like this: first you are given a job to do, and along with it you are taught the best way to do that job. Intellectual learning grows right alongside spiritual growth. There, people no longer struggle between body and spirit, so they can learn with a unified mind. Many people from Urantia will someday have the chance to serve as these overseers too.
p37.019 | 7. Mansion World Teachers
p37.020 | The Mansion World Teachers are glorified cherubim, angels who have reached a high stage. The mansion worlds are the worlds where people first wake up and learn after death, and these teachers handle most of the teaching there. There are so many of them that they cannot be counted. The later papers tell more about them.
p37.021 | 8. Higher Spirit Orders of Assignment
p37.022 | Higher spirits who belong to the family of the Infinite Spirit also stay in the local universe. The Solitary Messengers help others get past the limits of time and space. The circuit supervisor, Andovontia, isolated Urantia at the time of Caligastia’s betrayal, but he is waiting for the day when we return to the universe circuits. The census director, Salsatia, automatically knows when a being with a will is born or dies and keeps count of them. Universal Conciliators, Technical Advisers, Celestial Recorders, and Morontia Companions also work alongside them.
p37.023 | 9. Permanent Citizens of the Local Universe
p37.024 | The local universe, too, has permanent citizens who stay there a long time. On Salvington live the Susatia and the Spirit-fused mortals; on Edentia, the constellation headquarters, live the univitatia; and on Jerusem, the system capital, live the Material Sons and Daughters. The last beings the Creator Son makes are the Material Sons and Daughters, male and female, and these are the original Adam and Eve of each system. Some of them are sent out as Planetary Adams to uplift the human races of their worlds.
p37.025 | The permanent citizens of Urantia are the midway creatures. People are born, live a short time, and leave for other worlds, and the angels also stay only for a while, but the midwayers remain on the planet and keep working. So they can be called the real custodians of Urantia.
p37.026 | 10. Other Local Universe Groups
p37.027 | Nebadon has countless other beings too. The spirongia are spirit helpers who assist with the everyday spiritual work of Nebadon, and the spornagia are beings who look like perfect animals and tend the physical surroundings of the headquarters worlds. Ascending mortals who have reached Salvington also work in many places as assistants, students, and teachers, reaching out a hand to the companions who come after them.
p37.028 | This universe is a vast school in which the pilgrims of time move on from life to life and from world to world. At the end of the road, Paradise and an eternal assignment are waiting. When we know that so many beings are helping us, this time of learning and growing right now will feel all the more precious.
p38.001 | Introduction
p38.002 | The personalities of the Infinite Spirit come in three orders: angels, Messengers, and Powers. Angels are the ministering spirits of time, the Messengers are the messengers of space, and the Powers are the higher personalities of the Infinite Spirit. Just as the central universe has supernaphim and the superuniverses have seconaphim, in the local universe the seraphim, cherubim, and sanobim make up the angelic hosts. Seraphim look almost the same in every universe, and they are skilled workers who take care of many tasks in the local universe.
p38.003 | 1. Origin of Seraphim
p38.004 | Seraphim are created by the Universe Mother Spirit, and they are born 41,472 at a time. Around the time the Creator Son made the first Material Son, the Mother Spirit began for the first time to produce spirit children on her own, and that was the beginning of the seraphim. Before that, the seraphim working in Nebadon were angels borrowed for a while from a neighboring universe. Nebadon is still growing, so seraphim are still being born even now.
p38.005 | 2. Angelic Natures
p38.006 | Angels have no bodies, but they are real individuals. We cannot see angels, but angels see us just as we are. They understand human life, they share our feelings, and they enjoy music, art, and humor. They also know our moral struggles and spiritual difficulties well, and they love people. Angels are geniuses at calculation, so things that would be enormous tasks for us are very easy for them.
p38.007 | Angels are ahead of us spiritually, but they do not judge or accuse us. So we too should not be quick to judge other people. It is good to love angels, but we must not worship them. When a seer fell down before a seraphim, the angel said, “⟦Q 38:2.5⟧See that you do it not;⟦/Q⟧” and explained that the angel was a fellow servant who also serves God. When we lay aside our bodies, we will become very much like angels, and as we ascend we will become good friends with them.
p38.008 | 3. Unrevealed Angels
p38.009 | Many angels who are unknown to us also work in the local universe. The angels this paper talks about are only the seraphim and the groups close to them who help human beings survive and ascend. Besides these, six orders of unrevealed angels take care of the administration of Nebadon and other work, and they have no direct connection with human ascension.
p38.010 | 4. The Seraphic Worlds
p38.011 | The ninth group of worlds around Salvington is the worlds of the seraphim. These worlds are beautiful and spacious, and every seraphim has a real home there. Here a home means a place where two seraphim live together. Seraphim are not male and female, but they form pairs who complete each other, and they do most of their work as a pair. They are very affectionate, but they have no sex emotions. Every thousand years the seraphim hold a reunion.
p38.012 | 5. Seraphic Training
p38.013 | Seraphim spend their first thousand years on Salvington watching and learning, and the next thousand years in training on the seraphic worlds. After that they are assigned to a constellation, and they begin their service as observers on the lowest evolutionary worlds. Only after more study in the constellation and the system are they commissioned as ministering spirits of time. Once commissioned, seraphim travel throughout the universe, linking the spirit world with the people of the material worlds.
p38.014 | 6. Seraphic Organization
p38.015 | Seraphim are organized in twelves. Twenty-four seraphim form a group, twelve groups form a company, twelve companies form a battalion, twelve battalions form a unit, and twelve units form a legion. One legion is about half a million angels. This is what Jesus meant in the garden of Gethsemane when he said that if he asked, the Father would give him “⟦Q 38:6.1⟧more than twelve legions of angels.⟦/Q⟧”
p38.016 | A still larger organization, an angelic army, numbers more than seventy million. The one who leads all these angels is Gabriel, the “⟦Q 38:6.2⟧supreme commander of the armies of heaven,⟦/Q⟧” Since Michael came to Urantia, all the angels have been under his rule.
p38.017 | 7. Cherubim and Sanobim
p38.018 | Cherubim and sanobim are like seraphim, but they are the lowest order of angels, so they are closer to people. They are very intelligent and affectionate, almost human. Cherubim are the positive side and sanobim the complementing side, and they always work in pairs. They are faithful assistants who help the seraphim, but the work of a guardian angel who watches over one person is done only by seraphim.
p38.019 | When cherubim come to a planet, they learn its customs and languages and keep improving themselves. One out of four of them is semimaterial, so they are especially useful for work along the border between matter and spirit.
p38.020 | 8. Evolution of Cherubim and Sanobim
p38.021 | Cherubim and sanobim can grow too. Those among them who are ascension candidates can become seraphim through steady effort and experience, while others have limits to their growth. Experienced cherubim work as helpers to the guardians of destiny who watch over people, and when that seraphim leaves, they are embraced by the Mother Spirit and become Mansion World Teachers.
p38.022 | Those who serve a long time as Mansion World Teachers are embraced by the Mother Spirit once more and are born again as full seraphim. Then the way opens for them to become the guardian of destiny for a person and to go with that person all the way to Paradise. This shows that anyone who keeps serving and learning can grow higher.
p38.023 | 9. The Midway Creatures
p38.024 | Midway creatures are beings between humans and angels, and they are always found on life-experiment planets like Urantia. The primary midwayers came from the staff that helped the Planetary Prince. There are always fifty thousand of them, and they are more like angels. The secondary midwayers came from Adam and Eve or their descendants, and they are more like humans. They did not come about by accident; they were planned from the beginning.
p38.025 | The material world and the spirit world are linked by a ladder that runs from humans to secondary midwayers, primary midwayers, cherubim, and seraphim. On Urantia, because the Planetary Prince betrayed his trust and Adam failed, the midwayers have been gathered together and work under the direction of the planetary custodians. They are also the historians of the planet.
p38.026 | The midwayers stay on this world for a long time and help people. If they remain faithful to the end, they finally become ascending sons of God and set out with their earthly brothers and sisters, whom they have watched over, on the long adventure toward Paradise. It is very reassuring to know that so many beings are helping us where we cannot see.
p39.001 | Introduction
p39.002 | The Universe Mother Spirit means to create seraphim that are all equally perfect, but for some reason each seraphim turns out very different. After they finish their training, seraphim are sorted into seven groups: supreme, superior, supervisor, and administrator seraphim, planetary helpers, transition ministers, and seraphim of the future. This does not mean that one group is lower in worth than another.
p39.003 | Seraphim have no childhood, but like people they learn, gain experience, and grow in ability. Manotia, the seraphim who helped prepare this paper, has done all seven kinds of work through effort and service. Surprisingly, the more able an angel is, the more it wants to start from the lowest place, and angels especially want to help people on the planets.
p39.004 | 1. Supreme Seraphim
p39.005 | The supreme seraphim are the highest order of angels in the local universe. They help the higher Sons, and when a Paradise Son goes to a planet on a bestowal, 144 of them go along as his bestowal attendants. As court advisers they work to defend people, so that when justice weighs a wrong, mercy also fairly takes into account the person’s nature and circumstances.
p39.006 | They also work as guides who help those who are leaving the local universe for the superuniverse, as advisers to all teachers, and as recorders. Michael himself, on his fourth bestowal, served in the form of a seraphim teacher counselor.
p39.007 | 2. Superior Seraphim
p39.008 | The superior seraphim take care of the higher work of the local universe. There is the intelligence corps that gathers information for Gabriel; the voice of mercy, which encourages the good impulses of humans and angels; the spirit co-ordinators, who teach those just reborn as spirit beings; and the assistant teachers, recorders, and transporters. The text says that “⟦Q 39:2.5⟧Mercy is the keynote of seraphic service⟦/Q⟧”
p39.009 | Transporters carry those who cannot travel to other worlds on their own. Traveling while enfolded by a seraphim is something like sleeping. It is like falling asleep in one city and waking up in a faraway one: you fall asleep at a set time and wake at a set time. Angels cannot carry bodies of flesh and blood, so when we die we leave our bodies on this earth and receive a new morontia body on the mansion worlds, a body for the stage between matter and spirit.
p39.010 | 3. Supervisor Seraphim
p39.011 | The supervisor seraphim work in the constellations. As law forecasters, they show ahead of time how new laws will affect people’s lives, and they stand before the lawmakers to speak for those who cannot speak for themselves. The social architects help people who fit well together to gather and work as a group, and they guide them to form friendships based on true, honest understanding.
p39.012 | The ethical sensitizers help people grasp the morals of human relationships, and on Edentia they teach brotherhood. The transporters fly through space at about three times the speed of light, and they can change their own direction and speed.
p39.013 | 4. Administrator Seraphim
p39.014 | The administrator seraphim help govern the systems. The justice guides organize the evidence in trials where a person’s eternal destiny is at stake, so that justice is carried out fairly, together with mercy. At the time of the Lucifer rebellion, more than a quarter of the administrator seraphim were fooled by the false claims of freedom.
p39.015 | The ethical directors teach what loyalty is. We cannot receive so much and give nothing back, and only after we first learn loyalty, and then love, do we become truly free. They also teach that it is not good to stand still too much or to rush too much; we should keep moving forward steadily, one step at a time.
p39.016 | They say that life on this earth is the first link in a long chain that leads into eternity. Living this life through experience matters more than what we learn, and how we do a task matters more than what task we do. The text says, “⟦Q 39:4.14⟧The keys of the kingdom of heaven are: sincerity,⟦/Q⟧” Anyone who chooses to do the will of God is great.
p39.017 | 5. Planetary Helpers
p39.018 | The planetary helpers assist the Adam and Eve who come to a planet. On Urantia many helpers left after Adam failed, but some still remain and work here. The spirits of brotherhood help different races get along, and the souls of peace plant peace in a world full of conflict. Vevona, the angel who proclaimed “⟦Q 39:5.5⟧peace on earth and good will among men⟦/Q⟧” when Michael was born, was the chief of the souls of peace.
p39.019 | The spirits of trust plant faith in the minds of suspicious primitive people. They also help people see that not knowing the future actually becomes part of the joy of life and the charm of adventure. And the idea that angels have wings came from people seeing the energy shields of seraphim preparing for transport and thinking they were wings.
p39.020 | 6. Transition Ministers
p39.021 | The transition ministers help people through the process of changing from material beings into spiritual beings. In Satania right now, they mainly work on the seven mansion worlds, teaching those who have survived death. Among them are the seraphic evangels, the racial interpreters, the mind planners, and the morontia counselors, and the paper on the mansion worlds tells more about them.
p39.022 | 7. Seraphim of the Future
p39.023 | The seraphim of the future are not yet widely at work. Most of them are waiting near Salvington. They are angels being made ready for the day when the age of light and life dawns in Nebadon, the age when the whole world becomes spiritually mature. Because they have no direct connection with Urantia right now, a detailed description has been left out.
p39.024 | 8. Seraphic Destiny
p39.025 | The eternal goal of the seraphim is a world called Seraphington, which is the threshold through which angels enter Paradise. There are many ways for seraphim to reach Paradise, but the most precious way is to become the guardian of destiny who watches over one person to the very end. These angels lead people along the path of divine destiny and, while doing so, also fulfill their own high destiny.
p39.026 | Angels are created a little higher than human beings, so they cannot start from the very bottom. Instead, they come down to us, who do start at the bottom, and lead us step by step and world by world. No angel who has reached Seraphington has ever gone astray.
p39.027 | 9. The Corps of Seraphic Completion
p39.028 | The angels who enter the Seraphic Corps of Completion begin eternal service on Paradise, and many of them return to their home universes to work again. What they long for most is to go along when a Paradise Son comes in human form.
p39.029 | Even now these angels are pouring their whole hearts into helping people on the long journey of seeking God and going to Paradise. In every adventure of finding God, the angels are always our true friends and dependable helpers. We are never alone.

After serving several people one after another, or after the special experience of indwelling a bestowal Son, an Adjuster may receive personality from the Universal Father. Adjusters who are personalized in this way are the highest order of the ascending sons.

p40.012 | Human beings are the very last link among the beings called sons of God. The reach of the Eternal Son comes down through many steps all the way to one who is very much like us, Jesus. The reach of the Infinite Spirit extends to the angels, and the angels, created a little higher than we are, watch over us. The Father does not come to us in person, but he is within us as the Thought Adjuster, so the One who is farthest away is actually the closest.
p40.013 | Humans are divided into three groups according to their relationship with the Adjuster: people of primitive times, in whom the Adjuster stays only for a while; people of a special type who cannot fuse with the Adjuster; and people like us, who can fuse with the Adjuster. The first two groups gain eternal life by fusing with a spirit of the Mother Spirit.
p40.014 | Above all, one thing is certain: any soul that wants to cooperate with the Adjuster and has a desire to find God will survive, even if that desire is only the faintest first flicker toward the true light.
p40.015 | 6. The Faith Sons of God

p40.017 | First, we are faith sons who accept and believe in our sonship. Second, the Creator Son, Michael, became one of us and became our elder brother. Third, the spirit of the Son, the Spirit of Truth, has been poured out on all people. Fourth, the Father has given us personality with free will. Fifth, the Adjuster, a fragment of the Father, lives within us.
p40.018 | 7. Father-Fused Mortals
p40.019 | Human beings like us can become Father-fused mortals. The Adjuster is an immortal spirit, so when we fuse with the Adjuster, our soul gains eternal life. Through faith, and by freely cooperating with the leading of the Adjuster, we can rise to the very heights of glory. Just as the Son of God and the Son of Man were one in Jesus, we truly become ascending sons on the day we and our Adjuster become one forever.
p40.020 | The Father says, “⟦Q 40:7.4⟧Be you perfect, even as I am perfect.⟦/Q⟧” Because he has given himself to us, he expects perfection of us. Only those who have fused with the Adjuster pass through Havona and meet God on Paradise. Even for us, walking through an ordinary day right now, this amazing destiny lies ahead.

p40.022 | Most people fuse with their Adjusters on the mansion worlds or the morontia worlds. But in rare cases, some never fuse, even after many chances. If it is ruled that the person is not at fault, the Adjuster leaves and that person fuses with the spirit of the Creator Son. These are the Son-fused mortals, and there are fewer than a million of them in all of Orvonton. They serve mainly in the superuniverse, and they will be our good friends on the superuniverse training worlds.

Because they cannot take along the memories the Adjuster kept, they wake up without remembering their past life.
p40.025 | Even so, through the stories the angels tell and through the records, they can come to know their old life again. That is because the experience that remains deep in the soul responds to those stories and recognizes them, as if saying, Yes, that is how it was. Most of them become permanent citizens of the local universe, and the eighth group of worlds around Salvington is their home.
p40.026 | 10. Ascendant Destinies
p40.027 | Spirit-fused mortals stay in the local universe, Son-fused mortals stay in the superuniverse, and Adjuster-fused mortals go all the way to Paradise. That is because the spirit each one has fused with returns to the place it came from. This is not a mistake in the plan. The local universe and the superuniverse also need permanent citizens who have gathered long experience and wisdom. The deep wisdom of those who have served a long time in one universe is essential to that universe’s stability.
p40.028 | So we cannot say that one destiny is greater and another is lesser. The destinies are simply different. The Father loves each of his children equally, and with a love meant for that one person alone. The text says, “⟦Q 40:10.13⟧Sonship is the supreme relationship of the creature to the Creator.⟦/Q⟧” Let us remember that we belong to God’s family, and joyfully take hold of this amazing opportunity we have been given.
p41.001 | Introduction
p41.002 | Where does our local universe, Nebadon, begin, and where does it end? What sets Nebadon apart from everything else is not a wall you can see but the presence of the Universe Mother Spirit, the Divine Minister of Salvington. Nebadon reaches as far as her presence reaches, and beyond that lies another local universe. There are no clear physical boundary lines between local universes. The stars and planets of Nebadon were born from Andronover and several other nebulae (huge clouds of gas that give birth to stars and planets), but the power directors bound them together so that they move as one along the superuniverse orbit. This Nebadon star cloud is now circling the center in Sagittarius in an ever steadier way.
p41.003 | 1. The Nebadon Power Centers
p41.004 | When a nebula forms, the Paradise force organizers start the work first, and after that the power centers and physical controllers guide the physical growth of the stars and planets. When the Creator Son arrived, they built lines of communication and energy circuits to fit his plans and bound Nebadon together as a single unit. One hundred power centers stay on Salvington, receiving the energies that come from Uversa, the superuniverse headquarters, stepping them down and changing them, and sending them on to the constellations and systems. Ten are stationed in each constellation and one in each system. The power center of Satania is located on the dark island at the center of the system, a huge body that gives off no light. Interestingly, we are told that light and electricity are not the basic energies of space but only secondary forms of them.
p41.005 | 2. The Satania Physical Controllers
p41.006 | Our system, Satania, is one of the hundred systems in the constellation of Norlatiadek, and it is made up of more than seven thousand astronomical groups. On Jerusem, the system headquarters, is the chief of physical controllers, and half a million living, intelligent energy manipulators oversee the flow of energy throughout Satania.
p41.007 | Just as plants take in sunlight and turn it into the energy of life, life has the power to gather and change energy. The physical controllers are beings who handle energy at the stage before life, and they adjust their methods to suit the makeup of each planet. But they are not responsible for every energy irregularity on Urantia. Urantia is a small planet that sits on a huge energy line, so even the controllers have trouble holding back the powerful Norlatiadek currents.
p41.008 | 3. Our Starry Associates
p41.009 | More than two thousand suns shine in Satania, and our sun is an average star among them. Of the thirty suns closest to us, only three are brighter than our sun. The largest star, Antares, has a diameter four hundred and fifty times that of our sun, but the universe is wide enough to hold such giant suns with room to spare.
p41.010 | A sun that is too large splits in two and becomes a double star, and a sun that is too small cools off quickly. In 1572 the light of a double-star explosion so bright that it could be seen even in broad daylight reached Urantia. A star’s color tells its age: very young and very old stars are red, and a brilliant white glow means a star in its vigorous adult years. Variable stars, whose brightness changes, are even used as lighthouses of space for measuring great distances.
p41.011 | 4. Sun Density
p41.012 | Our sun is about one and a half times as dense as water, but it is a gas, neither liquid nor solid. That is because density is not about what state the atoms are in but about how much mass is packed into a space. So some suns can be as heavy as iron even though they are gas. p45.011 | 4. The Four and Twenty Counselors
p45.012 | On Jerusem are the four and twenty counselors, whom John the Revelator called the “⟦Q 45:4.1⟧four and twenty elders⟦/Q⟧”. They received their places about nineteen hundred years ago, soon after Michael became the full sovereign of Nebadon, and they act for Michael on the isolated worlds of Satania.
p45.013 | All of them come from Urantia. Among them are ancient leaders who led the races to true worship, Adam and Eve after their rehabilitation, Enoch, the first human to fuse with his Adjuster while still alive, Moses, Elijah, Machiventa Melchizedek, John the Baptist, and 1-2-3 the First, the leader of the loyal midway creatures. The other eight seats are being kept open for ascending mortals and great teachers of ages to come. It is moving to know that people who opened the way of faith on our planet hold such important work in heaven.
p45.014 | 5. The Material Sons
p45.015 | The place on Jerusem that draws the most interest from newcomers is the realm of the Material Sons and Daughters, the order of Adam and Eve. They are the highest type of beings who have material bodies, form families, and have children, and they are the last physical link in the chain of personality that runs from divinity down to humanity. There are more than one hundred sixty million of them in Nebadon.
p45.016 | They handle most of the everyday affairs of Jerusem, and they are experimenting with self-government, ruling themselves through elections and representative government. The Melchizedeks provide excellent education and spiritual training for the young Material Sons and Daughters.
p45.017 | 6. Adamic Training of Ascenders
p45.018 | The homes of the Material Sons and Daughters are very much like our human homes. So ascending mortals, by spending time with them, can fill in the experience of family life that they missed on earth.
p45.019 | The text says that no one can reach Paradise without the experience of being a parent who raises children, or some experience equal to it. That is because the relationship between parents and children is the foundation for understanding the relationship between the Universal Father and his children. Those who never became parents on earth gain this experience by living in the homes of the Material Sons on Jerusem and helping raise children, or in the nurseries of the finaliters’ world. Children who die young also receive their personalities again and grow up in these nurseries, and later they can choose the path of ascension for themselves. It is worth remembering that the hard work of the parents who raise us is treasured this much even in heaven.
p45.020 | 7. The Melchizedek Schools
p45.021 | The mansion worlds are schools of second chance for those who did not finish their spiritual growth on earth. People who died early, or whose growth fell short because of inborn weaknesses or hard circumstances, can keep learning here and make up what they lack, as long as they have a sincere heart. The Melchizedeks run more than thirty schools on Jerusem and teach ascenders to take on the responsibilities of representative government.
p45.022 | Every citizen of Jerusem has the right to vote, but one person’s vote counts from one to a thousand, depending on how much mota, or morontia wisdom, that person has gained. Ascenders are tested in turn on wisdom, spiritual insight, and the ability to get along with others. Only after they have developed a mota personality with all of these can they go on to the next stage, the constellation. Wisdom and character are what qualify a citizen of heaven.
p46.001 | Introduction
p46.002 | Jerusem, the headquarters of Satania, is an average local system capital, but because of the Lucifer rebellion and Michael’s bestowal on Urantia, it has a few special features. It once went through great trouble, but now order and good will have been restored, and it is being run very well. Interestingly, the text says that the heaven many believers today have pictured is really just such a system headquarters.
p46.003 | 1. Physical Aspects of Jerusem
p46.004 | A day on Jerusem lasts almost three days of Urantia time, and one hundred days make a year. There is no day and night, and there are no seasons. Energy is sent up into the sky and reflected back down from the ceiling of the atmosphere, so a soft, even light, like sunlight at ten o’clock in the morning, comes down from every direction. The temperature always stays around 70 degrees Fahrenheit. When three quarters of the day have passed, the light dims to about the brightness of a full moon, and this is the quiet time when all Jerusem rests. Jerusem gives off no light of its own, so even if it were very close, our eyes could not see it.
p46.005 | 2. Physical Features of Jerusem
p46.006 | Jerusem has no earthquakes and no rain, so instead of rugged mountain ranges it has beautiful highlands and countless little lakes. Instead of rain, dew falls during each time of dimmed light. A third gas is added to the atmosphere to suit the breathing of morontia beings. There is no struggle for survival and no preying of the strong on the weak; material life and morontia life blend beautifully together and give a foretaste of the glory of Paradise.
p46.007 | Jerusem is far more material than spiritual, so it even has amazing factories and laboratories, though there are no smoking chimneys. From Seraphic Mount, the highest point, a transport seraphim carrying passengers leaves for distant parts of the universe every three seconds, and those who arrive land on the sea of glass.
p46.008 | 3. The Jerusem Broadcasts
p46.009 | On Jerusem, the favorite thing ascending mortals like to do is listen to the universe broadcasts. The huge broadcast amphitheater can seat more than five billion material and morontia beings, and news keeps pouring in from Salvington, from Edentia, and from Uversa, the superuniverse headquarters. When a message comes from Paradise, all the residents gather on the sea of glass, and at those times everything that is heard can also be seen. In this way ascenders get a foretaste of the greater beauty they will meet in the future.
p46.010 | 4. Residential and Administrative Areas
p46.011 | Jerusem has administrative areas that look after the 619 inhabited worlds and 56 transition worlds of Satania, along with many residential areas. These areas are divided into circles, squares, rectangles, and triangles, and each has its own role. The system capital is the only world that has all three ways of existing, material, morontial, and spiritual, so beings of every kind feel at home here. There are also helpers like the spirongia, who work between material beings and spirit beings. The author says he cannot fully describe the grandeur of this place, and yet Jerusem is only the first step on the road to Paradise.
p46.012 | 5. The Jerusem Circles
p46.013 | The circles are residential areas, shaped like rings one inside another, where many groups of beings live. The Sons of God, the angels, Universe Aids such as the Evening Stars, the physical controllers, the ascending mortals, the various colonies, and the finaliters each live in their own seven-ringed round areas. The outer rings are raised so that they look down on the inner ones, and from the walkways on top of walls made of crystal gems, anyone can watch their activities.
p46.014 | In the center of the area of the Sons is the Michael memorial, finished about five hundred years ago. Michael himself came for its dedication, and all Jerusem heard the story of his bestowal on Urantia, the least of the worlds. Around the area of the angels is a long exhibit hall showing how the worlds of Satania are progressing, and in the area of the ascending mortals each planet has its own memorial, so the Urantia memorial is still changing even now.
p46.015 | Life here is made up of work, learning, and rest, that is, service, study, and relaxation. In the area of the finaliters there is an empty temple sealed with Michael’s seal and inscribed “⟦Q 46:5.32⟧Undedicated⟦/Q⟧”, and no one yet knows its mystery.
p46.016 | 6. The Executive-Administrative Squares
p46.017 | The administration of the system is carried out in one thousand squares, which are grouped into ten large departments. These include the management of physical energy, justice and ethics, planetary affairs, education, science, morontia affairs, spiritual activities, ascender ministry, and grand universe philosophy. Amazingly, all these buildings are transparent, so that even visiting students can see every activity of the system. It is a picture of a government run openly, with nothing to hide.
p46.018 | 7. The Rectangles—The Spornagia
p46.019 | In the rectangles live the spornagia, the native life of Jerusem. They are the gardeners of the headquarters world. They make the land beautiful and serve every being that needs material help. The spornagia have no Adjusters and no surviving souls, but they can live for more than forty thousand years, and when their bodies wear out, they receive new bodies and live again. They are the only beings in Nebadon like this.
p46.020 | The author describes them as being like a faithful horse and a loving dog combined, more intelligent than the smartest chimpanzee, and very beautiful. They cannot understand worship, but out of affection for their masters they take part in spiritual devotions. Some believe that someday they too will reach a higher growth.
p46.021 | 8. The Jerusem Triangles
p46.022 | The everyday affairs of Jerusem are handled in one hundred triangles. Around them is a panorama picturing the history of Jerusem, and more than two miles of it, the part connected with the Lucifer rebellion, are blank. This part will be restored when the judgment on the rebellion is finished and Satania returns to the constellation. When that time comes, isolated worlds like Urantia will be able to communicate with other worlds again.
p46.023 | God is merciful and very patient, but evil that is chosen knowingly and stubbornly surely comes to an end. The text closes this paper with the words, “⟦Q 46:8.4⟧The wages of sin is death⟦/Q⟧” It makes us think together about how important our choices are, and how beautiful a world awaits those who choose the good.
p47.001 | Introduction
p47.002 | When Jesus was on earth, he spoke of the “⟦Q 47:0.1⟧many mansions in the Father's universe.⟦/Q⟧” The mansion worlds are the seven training worlds where we first wake up after death and then learn and grow. They are the seven satellites of the first transition world circling Jerusem, the finaliters’ world. Finaliters are beings who have already completed the Paradise ascent. The morontia supervisors and the Melchizedeks are in charge of the mansion worlds, and many other beings, such as artists, teachers, and helpers, work there with them.
p47.003 | 1. The Finaliters’ World
p47.004 | We cannot yet see the temple of the finaliters’ world, but now and then, with the help of the energy transformers, we can catch a glimpse of the finaliters. Knowing that brothers and sisters who finished the long journey ahead of us have come back to the place where it began is a great encouragement that we too can go all the way.
p47.005 | On the first mansion world, everyone goes through a review by the parental commission. This commission is made up of twelve couples who have each raised three or more children to the age of puberty. No one can skip the experience of raising children, and the text stresses that fathers need this experience just as much as mothers do. Children need their fathers as much as their mothers.
p47.006 | 2. The Probationary Nursery
p47.007 | On the finaliters’ world there are nurseries that raise children who died young. If even one of the parents survives and ascends, the child wakes up here when that parent arrives on the first mansion world. The children are grouped by age into families of five and grow up surrounded by love, and parents can visit up to four times a year to hold their children. The text calls these meetings one of the most touching scenes in the whole ascending career.
p47.008 | As the children grow up, they are given the chance to choose for themselves. If they choose the path to Paradise, they move up to the mansion worlds and begin their ascent. If they finally refuse, they are not punished; it becomes as if they had never existed. God respects the right of choice even for a little child.
p47.009 | 3. The First Mansion World
p47.010 | In the center of the first mansion world is the resurrection hall. When our memories and mind, kept by the Thought Adjuster, and our soul, held in trust by the guardian angel, are brought back together, we wake up in a new body. After waking, we are given ten days of free time to look around our new home and to visit family members and friends who arrived before us.
p47.011 | What matters is that we start again at exactly the place where we stopped when we died. The text says, “⟦Q 47:3.7⟧You begin over there right where you leave off down here.⟦/Q⟧” Dying does not make anyone wise automatically. On the first mansion world, the main work is to correct the defects of character that formed on earth and to fill in the experiences we missed. The morontia companions stay beside us as friendly companions and guides to help.
p47.012 | 4. The Second Mansion World
p47.013 | On the second mansion world, communities and working groups form, and mansion life really begins. Here there is none of the prejudice or class discrimination found in human society. Each time we move to another world, we fall asleep and wake up in a new body, but because the Adjuster is with us, all our memories of value carry on.
p47.014 | Even in morontia bodies we eat, drink, and rest, but the food is living energy, so nothing is left over as waste. As we move up world by world, we become less material, more intellectual, and a little more spiritual. On the second world, in particular, inner conflicts and mental disharmonies are healed.
p47.015 | 5. The Third Mansion World
p47.016 | The third mansion world is the headquarters of the Mansion World Teachers. They are the kind teachers to whom we will say our last goodbyes when we leave the local universe. If the first two worlds were places for making up what we lacked, active learning begins from the third world on. Here ascenders start linking mota, morontia wisdom, with human philosophy, and they begin to understand the meaning of the universe more deeply.
p47.017 | 6. The Fourth Mansion World
p47.018 | By the time we reach the fourth mansion world, we have come a long way from material life. Here ascenders learn real social life for the first time. This society is not built on getting ahead or beating others but on sympathy that understands one another, on loving service that expects nothing in return, and on the shared goal of reaching God together. Every ascender finds joy in knowing God, revealing God, and seeking God.
p47.019 | 7. The Fifth Mansion World
p47.020 | The fifth mansion world is a big leap forward in the ascending career. After the local universe language, ascenders learn the superuniverse language of Uversa, and they get ready for the next stage, life in the constellation.
p47.021 | Above all, it is here that cosmic consciousness is truly born. As their outlook widens, they begin to realize that an amazing and magnificent destiny awaits those who go all the way to Paradise. The text says that at this point “⟦Q 47:7.5⟧Study is becoming voluntary, unselfish service natural, and worship spontaneous.⟦/Q⟧” When the things we once did only because we had to become joys, that is what real growth looks like.
p47.022 | 8. The Sixth Mansion World
p47.023 | On the sixth mansion world, in many cases, the human mind fully fuses with the Thought Adjuster. At that moment, in front of the ascender’s friends, the messengers proclaim, “⟦Q 47:8.4⟧This is a beloved son in whom I am well pleased.⟦/Q⟧” With this simple ceremony the ascender enters the eternal career, receives a new name, and spends forty days quietly looking back and choosing the road ahead.
p47.024 | The higher we go on these worlds, the smaller the shadow of our animal nature becomes, and the more lovable the ascenders become. Those who have come up through great difficulties become very kind, understanding, and generously accepting of others.
p47.025 | 9. The Seventh Mansion World
p47.026 | The seventh mansion world is the highest achievement of the life after death. Here the difference between those who came from isolated, backward worlds and those who came from advanced worlds practically disappears. Every trace of unfortunate heredity and bad environment is washed away. Here ascenders visit the world of the Father and begin offering a new and more spiritual worship to the invisible Father.
p47.027 | Now the ascenders get ready to graduate together to Jerusem. Some may even choose to stay longer to wait for a friend who has fallen behind. When we leave, seen off by our companions gathered on the sea of glass, we are no longer going as guests; we are going to become citizens of Jerusem.
p47.028 | 10. Jerusem Citizenship
p47.029 | When the graduates of the mansion worlds arrive, all Jerusem gathers to welcome them. John saw this scene and wrote that those who had overcome their beastlike nature stood on the sea of glass, “⟦Q 47:10.2⟧having the harps of God,⟦/Q⟧” and sang the song of deliverance. This harp is a morontia device that lets them receive universe communications. Once they become citizens of Jerusem, there are no more resurrections of falling asleep and waking again, and their memory continues without a break.
p47.030 | Death is the way we leave the material body, and the mansion worlds are the introduction to the morontia life. We do not go to heaven in a perfect state; we are invited to keep growing, one step at a time, from wherever we stop now. So every effort we make today to learn and grow is never wasted.
