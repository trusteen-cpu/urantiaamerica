## p12.001
Introduction
## p12.002
This paper explains how vast the Universal Father’s creation is, so big we cannot imagine it. Even though our limited human minds cannot fully understand all of it, we can learn how the universe is put together and managed. This universe is eternal, and countless superuniverses and intelligent beings are in it. All the physical creation in the universe has limits, yet its potential is still infinite, and it keeps on growing. In short, the universe is so big and complex it seems endless, but within it there is order and purpose.
## p12.003
1. Space Levels of the Master Universe
## p12.004
The universe may seem endless, but in fact it has limits. The universe is divided into several layers around its center, Paradise, and each layer has a different size and role. The universe at the center is the perfect universe called ‘Havona,’ an eternal place that does not change. Around Havona are seven superuniverses, and our own superuniverse, ‘Orvonton,’ is one of them. These superuniverses are evolving, and it is said that all their energy comes from Paradise. The superuniverses are apart from one another, yet everything is connected, moving around within great energy circuits. The size of this space and the motions going on in it are on a scale we can hardly imagine. Describing the universe layer by layer like this may feel complicated, but everything is connected and moves in good order.
## p12.005
2. The Domains of the Unqualified Absolute
## p12.006
The Unqualified Absolute refers to the place where vast space and energy, beyond what we can easily imagine, are gathered. When astronomers look at outer space with powerful telescopes, they find many universes, not yet finished, growing quickly. These outer universes exist beyond the superuniverses we know, places where no living beings such as humans or angels live yet. The Unqualified Absolute is where tremendous force and energy are at work in space, but not everything about it has been revealed. We do not know well how this energy moves or what changes it brings about. It is thought that these outer universes keep growing because they are part of a still greater creation to come.
## p12.007
3. Universal Gravity
## p12.008
In the universe there is a power that draws everything, called ‘gravity.’ This gravity is divided into four main kinds. The first is ‘the personality gravity of the Father,’ the power that draws all personalities in the universe. The second is ‘the spirit gravity of the Eternal Son,’ the power by which all spiritual beings are connected. The third is ‘the mind gravity of the Conjoint Actor,’ the power that connects all minds. Last, ‘the cosmic gravity of the Isle of Paradise’ is the absolute power that draws all matter. These powers are independent of one another, yet at the same time they affect one another and keep the universe going. All matter, spirit, and mind are kept in order by this gravity, and it has an important part in keeping the universe in balance.
## p12.009
4. Space and Motion
## p12.010
Everything in the universe is always moving. Space is not standing still; it is turning around Paradise. When we look, stars and planets may seem to be standing still, but in truth they keep moving. This motion is not simply moving from place to place; it is the energy of the universe continually circulating. The universe expands and contracts as if it were breathing, and this motion goes on as the space and energy of the universe stay in harmony. Our solar system, too, takes part in this huge cosmic motion, and everything is moving together in one great rotation.
## p12.011
5. Space and Time
## p12.012
Time and space cannot be separated in the world where we live. As long as we live, time and space are always working together. For example, when we move, we sense time, and we remember when something happened. But on Paradise, time works differently. Because Paradise is an eternal place, there is a timeless kind of existence, unlike the time we feel. Here is another interesting fact: the human mind can sometimes imagine freely, without being held back by space and time. Still, for us time remains an important idea, and within its flow we experience and learn.
## p12.013
6. Universal Overcontrol
## p12.014
The whole universe does not just happen by chance; it is controlled under a well-designed plan. This control is not simply physical laws or gravity; it is a united effort that the Universal Father, the Eternal Son, and the Conjoint Actor are carrying out together. In the universe, physical energy, mind, and spiritual forces work together in harmony. Thanks to this control, the universe can stay stable. Just as a clock runs with many parts fitting together exactly, the universe is run with many elements working together in harmony.
## p12.015
7. The Part and the Whole
## p12.016
In the universe, each individual and the whole are both important. God loves each person one by one, but at the same time he thinks of everyone as one connected family. This means that the whole universe is connected together. When one person makes a wrong choice, it affects other people too. In the same way, when one person does something good, it has a good effect on others as well. So the part and the whole affect each other and grow together. In the universe, each individual is independent and at the same time has an important role as part of the whole.
## p12.017
8. Matter, Mind, and Spirit
## p12.018
The universe is made up of matter, mind, and spirit. Matter is everything we can see with our eyes, and mind is the power to think and feel. Spirit goes beyond all of these and makes up our true nature. Matter is moved by the force that comes from Paradise, and spirit is what the Eternal Son gives to living beings. And what connects these two is mind. Mind links matter and spirit, and it helps us understand and recognize the world. Although we live in a material world, through mind and spirit we come to realize higher truth and reach spiritual growth.
## p12.019
9. Personal Realities
## p12.020
Personality is a reality that matters more than anything material. Our personality is formed as matter, mind, and spirit work together in harmony. Personality is not just something you can see; the personality God has given us does not change. It is unchanging, like our identity, but it shows itself more fully as we experience and grow. Relationships between people, and love, have an important part in the growth of personality. Love happens through the connection between personalities, and it gives us the power to understand and accept each other. Not everything can be known by analyzing it, as in science or mathematics, but we come to understand the reality of personality little by little through our relationships and experiences with one another.
## p13.001
Introduction
## p13.002
Between the central Isle of Paradise and the innermost planets of the Havona universe there are special sacred spheres. These spheres are arranged in three circuits, and each circuit is made up of seven worlds connected with the Father, the Son, and the Spirit. All twenty-one of these worlds have existed from eternity, and special spiritual energy shines out from these spheres and lights up the universe. These sacred spheres are mysterious places that we know little about, and each one carries out its own role and function. The Father’s worlds are known as the final destination for personal beings, and the Son’s worlds are known as places where impersonal beings stay. Because we have very little information about life on these spheres, it is hard to fully understand their nature and function.
## p13.003
1. The Seven Sacred Worlds of the Father
## p13.004
The seven sacred worlds of the Father are special places directly connected with the Universal Father. These worlds hold secrets that have to do with personality, so they are closed to visitors. The first world is Divinington, which is called ‘the bosom of the Father,’ the place where fragments of the Father such as Thought Adjusters gather. Here are kept the secrets of how Thought Adjusters work and how they are bestowed. The second world is Sonarington, called ‘the bosom of the Son,’ where the Sons of God stay. This place holds the secret of the incarnation of the divine Sons, that is, of how God is born as a human being. Besides these, each world has its own secrets and roles, and they are mysterious realms we cannot enter.
## p13.005
2. Father-World Relationships
## p13.006
These sacred worlds are places where different kinds of spirit beings stay and work. They are not just places to live; they are places where beings gain many kinds of spiritual experience and share in divine fellowship. For example, Ascendington, where ascending creatures stay, is a place that forms an important part of the spiritual journey of mortals as they rise to Paradise. Before mortals arrive on Paradise, it becomes a center of the activities they will experience in Havona. Each world at Paradise has a sacred role, and the beings there work according to their origin and their role. Mortals can visit some of the worlds of Paradise, but some of them will remain secrets we can never enter.
## p13.007
3. The Sacred Worlds of the Eternal Son
## p13.008
The seven worlds of the Eternal Son are worlds of pure spirit that give off light and spiritual energy. No personalities live on these worlds; they are mainly places where impersonal spirit beings are active. We do not know much about these worlds, but the energy produced there affects Havona, the central universe, and the superuniverses. The light produced on these worlds is a special light without heat, which lights up Havona and Paradise and supplies spiritual energy. Paradise philosophers think these spheres prepare spirit beings who will minister in the new universes of outer space. These worlds are very mysterious, and they are important places where impersonal beings are at work.
## p13.009
4. The Worlds of the Infinite Spirit
## p13.010
The seven worlds of the Infinite Spirit lie between the inner orbit of Havona and the spheres of the Eternal Son. On these worlds mainly the offspring of the Infinite Spirit are active, and they have an important role in keeping the balance of mind and spirit in the grand universe. Here the Seven Master Spirits manage the grand universe and supply spiritual energy to the superuniverses. On these worlds many levels of material, intellectual, and spiritual activity take place, and this complex activity is one of the most interesting sights on Paradise. Here many kinds of spirit beings work together to manage the universe, and physical, spiritual, and intellectual energies work in harmony. These worlds have a very important role as a center of all universe activity.
## p14.001
Introduction
## p14.002
At the very center of all creation is a perfect and divine universe. At its center is the huge motionless Isle of Paradise, and around it circles the central universe, made up of a billion worlds. The name of this central universe is Havona. Havona is very far away from Nebadon, our local universe, and its size and beauty are far greater than the human mind can measure.
## p14.003
Havona is not a universe that grew little by little over a long time; it is a universe that was made perfect from the beginning. All around it, countless universes are carrying on the great experiment and adventure of evolution. The Creator Sons of God take this perfect pattern universe as their model and work hard so that the universes in their care will one day become perfect like it.
## p14.004
1. The Paradise-Havona System
## p14.005
Going outward from Paradise, there are several bands, one after another. Innermost is a zone of quiet space, and beyond it the three circuits of the Paradise satellites and the seven circuits of the Havona worlds go around clockwise. The billion worlds of Havona are divided among seven round rings: the innermost ring has more than thirty-five million worlds, and the outermost ring has more than two hundred and forty-five million. Each circuit has a Circuit Spirit, who represents the Infinite Spirit, and who co-ordinates the work there.
## p14.006
Time is not measured on Paradise, but each of the Havona worlds has its own time. The worlds of the innermost circuit take almost a thousand years to go once around Paradise, and this is the Paradise-Havona standard day, that is, one day. So the Bible saying “⟦Q 14:1.12⟧A day is as a thousand years with God⟦/Q⟧” turned out, without anyone expecting it, to hold the truth.
## p14.007
On the outside of Havona, countless dark gravity bodies swirl around. These huge dark masses neither reflect nor absorb light, and they screen Havona. The inner band turns counterclockwise and the outer band clockwise, evening out gravity and keeping the central universe steady and unshaken. Nothing like this exists anywhere else in the whole universe.
## p14.008
2. Constitution of Havona
## p14.009
Spirit beings, too, live not in some cloudlike place but on real worlds. The Havona worlds are also made of real matter, but its makeup is different from the matter of our universe. The energy of our universe has two kinds of qualities, negative and positive, but the energy of Havona is made up of three. Havona matter is made of exactly one thousand elements, and the people who live there have forty-nine senses. So if we went to Havona just as we are now, we would not be able to see or hear anything.
## p14.010
In Havona, the spirit gravity of the Eternal Son and the mind gravity of the Infinite Spirit strongly draw every being toward Paradise. The beings here are more mature spiritually, so their desire to go to God is much stronger. Amazingly, in Havona sin has never once appeared. Both the beings who have always lived there and the beings who came in as pilgrims have all stayed loyal to God.
## p14.011
3. The Havona Worlds
## p14.012
There is no government in Havona. No courts and no lawmaking councils are needed. Because the beings there are already perfect or nearly perfect, there is no need to rule them with rules. But on each world there is one personality from the Trinity, called an Eternal of Days, who leads and teaches that world with outstanding wisdom.
## p14.013
The billion worlds of Havona are training worlds for high beings and also the final testing ground that pilgrims coming up from the worlds of time must pass through. The pilgrims arrive first on the seventh, outermost circuit, and they move inward one step at a time, from world to world and from circuit to circuit. The buildings, light, and decorations of the Havona worlds are far beyond human imagination, yet it is said that there are real rivers and lakes there too.
## p14.014
4. Creatures of the Central Universe
## p14.015
In Havona there are seven basic forms of life, from material, morontial, and spiritual beings up to absolute beings. Morontia means the stage between material and spiritual life. Here no one grows old and weak or dies. Even the lower living things do not die; they only change their form.
## p14.016
The natives of Havona came from the Paradise Trinity without parents, and they do not have offspring. They live to carry out the ideals of the Trinity, much as we try to do God’s will. Where people rejoice in God’s goodness, the Havoners rejoice in God’s beauty. Havona is also home to the pattern beings after whom every being in the whole universe is modeled, so the ideal form of us mortals is there too.
## p14.017
5. Life in Havona
## p14.018
We go through a short but intense testing on Earth, pass on through the mansion worlds, the local universe, and the superuniverse, and finally reach Havona. A pilgrim who arrives in Havona starts in the seventh circuit. On coming to know the Master Spirit of the superuniverse, the pilgrim moves on to the sixth; on knowing the Infinite Spirit, to the fourth; on knowing the Eternal Son, to the third; and on recognizing the Universal Father, to the second circuit. Then, passing through the first circuit, the pilgrim at last enters Paradise and becomes one of the Corps of the Finality.
## p14.019
Of the billion worlds of Havona, no two are alike. Because every world is original and perfect in its own way, there is no boredom there, only endless surprise and wonder. All the rules of Havona are natural and fair, so when you go there, you simply come to enjoy doing things the right way.
## p14.020
The love of adventure, curiosity, and dislike of boredom that are in us were not given to us to bother us. They are a sign telling us that death is not the end but “⟦Q 14:5.10⟧the beginning of an endless career of adventure⟦/Q⟧”. Right now these feelings are often blocked and we meet disappointments, but one day they will be gloriously fulfilled.
## p14.021
6. The Purpose of the Central Universe
## p14.022
Havona gives different joys and meanings to different beings. The Universal Father receives the worship of perfect creatures and feels great satisfaction as a parent, and he rejoices in Havona as the perfect model for all the universes. For the Eternal Son, Havona is the first proof that the Son is the Word of the Father; for the Infinite Spirit, it is the stage where he first practiced the ministry of mercy, and a graduate school for training minds. Havona also gives a preview of the perfect spirit nature of the Supreme Being.
## p14.023
The Creator Sons prepare in Havona to make universes, and the Universe Mother Spirits are trained here too. And for mortals like us, Havona is the goal we must reach before we enter Paradise. “⟦Q 14:6.39⟧Paradise is the home, and Havona the workshop and playground, of the finaliters⟦/Q⟧”. Even in the far future, Havona will go on being used as a training universe for new beings. When we think that the curiosity and dreams in our hearts will one day blossom there, even today’s learning becomes the first step of an eternal journey.
## p15.001
Introduction
## p15.002
Seen through the eyes of the Paradise Trinity, there are only seven inhabited universes outside Havona. These are called the seven superuniverses. All creation is like a great wheel. The hub at the center of the wheel is the Isle of Paradise, the seven spokes are the influences reaching out from the Seven Master Spirits of Paradise, and the rim of the wheel is the superuniverses. Each superuniverse is ruled by rulers called the Ancients of Days. This paper first describes the physical form of the superuniverses, and then shows their government and organization.
## p15.003
1. The Superuniverse Space Level
## p15.004
Our Earth and solar system are not flying off just anywhere without a map or a compass. The seven superuniverses move in good order, counterclockwise, along a huge elongated circle around Paradise and Havona. Our superuniverse, Orvonton, is the seventh superuniverse, and right now it has just rounded the southeastern bend and is racing along the long northern stretch. Someday we will pass again along the path our ancestors traveled.
## p15.005
2. Organization of the Superuniverses
## p15.006
The superuniverses are organized step by step, like bricks stacked one on another. About a thousand worlds where life can live make up one system; a hundred systems make a constellation; and a hundred constellations make a local universe. A hundred local universes form a minor sector, a hundred minor sectors form a major sector, and ten major sectors form a superuniverse. So one superuniverse has about one trillion inhabitable worlds, and all seven superuniverses together have about seven trillion. Of course, new systems keep coming into being, so these numbers are only approximate.
## p15.007
3. The Superuniverse of Orvonton
## p15.008
Almost every star we can see in the night sky belongs to Orvonton. The Milky Way is the central part of Orvonton. Our minor sector circles around the dense star clouds of Sagittarius. The Earth circles the sun, the solar system circles the center of the old Andronover nebula, and on top of that more layers of circling are added. These many layers of motion are one of the reasons astronomers get confused when they look at the sky.
## p15.009
4. Nebulae—The Ancestors of Universes
## p15.010
Nebulae are like mother wheels that give birth to stars. The Paradise force organizers start huge whirls of force in space, which gradually become matter and give birth to suns, in forms such as the spiral nebulae. One nebula has already given birth to forty thousand suns, and some of the great nebulae in distant space give birth to as many as a hundred million suns. The light of the Andromeda nebula, which you can see even with the naked eye, left there almost a million years ago.
## p15.011
5. The Origin of Space Bodies
## p15.012
Suns and planets come into being in many different ways. A nebula may gather together in rings, or it may whirl around and throw off suns. Our solar system came about when a huge star passed near the sun, and its gravity made matter pour out of the sun, which then gathered into planets. Besides these, there are planets formed as floating debris slowly clumped together, planets formed by collisions, and architectural worlds, like Salvington and Uversa, built to a design for special purposes.
## p15.013
6. The Spheres of Space
## p15.014
The bodies of space can be sorted into suns, dark islands, small bodies such as comets and meteors, planets, and architectural spheres. Suns work like power plants, taking in energy and giving it out again. Orvonton has more than ten trillion suns, and the whole universe has as many suns as there are glasses of water in the oceans of the Earth. The dark islands, with no light and no heat, hold neighboring systems with their heavy gravity and keep them in balance. A comet’s tail pointing away from the sun is proof that even light has weight. The Earth is a planet whose size, density, and location make it suitable for human life in many ways.
## p15.015
7. The Architectural Spheres
## p15.016
The headquarters of the superuniverses and the local universes are specially built architectural spheres. These worlds have their own light, which shines without heat. The place where we first wake up after death is the seven mansion worlds next to Jerusem, the headquarters of the system of Satania. After that we pass through Edentia, Salvington, Uminor the third, and Umajor the fifth, until we reach Uversa, the superuniverse headquarters; and after finishing our last preparations on the university worlds around it, we set out for Havona.
## p15.017
8. Energy Control and Regulation
## p15.018
The superuniverse headquarters, the power centers, and the physical controllers regulate the energy of space so that it flows evenly. When too much matter gathers in one place, huge collisions happen and change it back into energy. The farther you go from Paradise, the harder it is to predict what happens in the universe, and this means that there is a greater overcontrol above everything. Nebulae may scatter, suns may burn out, and planets may vanish, but the universe itself does not wear out.
## p15.019
9. Circuits of the Superuniverses
## p15.020
Many circuits flow through the superuniverses. A circuit is a pathway along which energy and influence come and go. The personality gravity of the Father, the spirit gravity of the Son, the mind gravity of the Conjoint Actor, and the material gravity of Paradise reach everywhere. The superuniverses have their own circuits, such as the mind circuit of the Master Spirit, and the local universes have the circuits of the Spirit of Truth and the Holy Spirit. When a local universe becomes physically stable and everyone in it becomes loyal to the Sovereign Son of that universe, it can join the superuniverse federation. Sadly, our Nebadon has not yet met those conditions.
## p15.021
10. Rulers of the Superuniverses
## p15.022
Each superuniverse is ruled jointly by three Ancients of Days. Working beside them are the Perfectors of Wisdom, the Divine Counselors, and the Universal Censors, who came directly from the Trinity. And the Mighty Messengers, Those High in Authority, and Those without Name and Number are beings who were once mortals, rose all the way to Paradise, and then took on this work. It is amazing that beings with the same origin as ours hold places in the government of the universe. The seven superuniverses have no ambassadors to one another; they hear news of one another only by way of Paradise.
## p15.023
11. The Deliberative Assembly
## p15.024
On Uversa there is also an assembly that deliberates about laws. Here perfect beings and beings who have evolved upward meet face to face. The representatives in the assembly are chosen from among the ascending pilgrims who are waiting to leave for Havona. In the history of Orvonton so far, the rulers and the assembly have never once come into conflict. This is proof that evolving beings, too, can become wise enough to work together with perfect beings.
## p15.025
12. The Supreme Tribunals
## p15.026
The superuniverses also have courts that handle trials. The Censors present the evidence, and the Mighty Messengers defend the children of time. The Sons who rule the local universes can grant people survival, but only the Ancients of Days can give a verdict that ends a being’s eternal life. The verdicts of this supreme court are always unanimous, and there is no further appeal from them.
## p15.027
13. The Sector Governments
## p15.028
The major sectors are ruled by the Perfections of Days, and the minor sectors by the Recents of Days. The major sector governments mainly look after intellectual matters, and the intellectual training of ascenders takes place on the seventy worlds around their headquarters. The minor sector governments are mainly in charge of physical stability and co-ordination. Reports from the Trinity Observers who stay in the constellations and local universes go up through the minor and major sectors all the way to the Ancients of Days.
## p15.029
14. Purposes of the Seven Superuniverses
## p15.030
Each of the seven superuniverses shows one purpose most clearly. Our Orvonton is famous for the merciful ministry it lavishly bestows on mortals. “⟦Q 15:14.2⟧Orvonton is a universe demonstration of love and mercy⟦/Q⟧”. Our Earth, Urantia, is planet number 606 in the system of Satania; Satania belongs to the constellation of Norlatiadek, and Norlatiadek belongs to the local universe of Nebadon.
## p15.031
The Earth is only one among an almost endless number of worlds. Even so, the Earth is governed with care and looked after with love, as if it were the only world in the whole universe. Even when I feel very small in this vast universe, I can remember that God is caring for each person, one by one, just like that.
## p16.001
Introduction
## p16.002
The Seven Master Spirits of Paradise are the first seven beings that the Infinite Spirit personalized. There are seven possible ways to combine the three persons, the Father, the Son, and the Spirit: the Father, the Son, and the Spirit each alone; the Father and the Son; the Father and the Spirit; the Son and the Spirit; and all three together. Each of the Seven Master Spirits came from one of these seven combinations. That is why the universe is run in seven parts, and why the number seven became the basis of universe organization.
## p16.003
The Seven Master Spirits are alike in spiritual character, but in every other way they are clearly different from one another. The most important work they do now is to look after the seven superuniverses, one each. Each Master Spirit has headquarters on the edge of Paradise and watches over its superuniverse from a place facing it.
## p16.004
1. Relation to Triune Deity
## p16.005
Because there are three persons, the Father, the Son, and the Spirit, there had to be seven combinations, so the appearing of the Seven Master Spirits was almost unavoidable. Through these seven Spirits, the Father, the Son, or the Spirit, or two of them together, can work in the universe. When the Seven Master Spirits gather together on Paradise, they together show the power, wisdom, and authority of the Trinity to the evolving universes.
## p16.006
2. Relation to the Infinite Spirit
## p16.007
The Father speaks through the Son, and the Father and the Son work together through the Infinite Spirit. And outside Paradise and Havona, the Infinite Spirit speaks only through the voices of the Seven Master Spirits. So the influence of the Infinite Spirit on a world takes on a slightly different coloring, according to the character of the Master Spirit in charge there. When the Seven Master Spirits are together, they show the all-powerfulness, all-knowingness, and everywhere-presence of the Infinite Spirit, but each one uses that power only within its own superuniverse.
## p16.008
3. Identity and Diversity of the Master Spirits
## p16.009
The Seven Master Spirits have names, but here they are introduced by number. Master Spirit Number One especially resembles the Universal Father, Number Two the Eternal Son, and Number Three the Infinite Spirit. Number Four resembles the Father and the Son together, Number Five the Father and the Spirit, and Number Six the Son and the Spirit. Each looks after the superuniverse with the same number and rules it in the way that the one it resembles would.
## p16.010
Master Spirit Number Seven resembles the Father, the Son, and the Spirit in equal measure. So Master Spirit Number Seven shows the spiritual form of the Supreme Being best of all, and is the natural chairman of the council of the Seven Master Spirits. Because the pilgrims of Havona cannot yet meet the Supreme Being directly, in the meantime Master Spirit Number Seven becomes for them one as close as God. It is this Master Spirit Number Seven who is in charge of our superuniverse, Orvonton.
## p16.011
4. Attributes and Functions of the Master Spirits
## p16.012
The Seven Master Spirits work on every level of the universe except the absolute levels. They made the power directors, who handle the physical energies of the universe, and they also give great help to the Creator Sons when they set up the local universes. It also seems that they had a large part in making the morontia worlds, the stage between matter and spirit. The Master Spirit of Orvonton is very likely to have an influence on things such as the beginning of life, the activation of mind, and the coming of the Holy Spirit and the Spirit of Truth. But it has nothing to do with the Thought Adjusters, the gift of spirit that God sends to the human mind.
## p16.013
5. Relation to Creatures
## p16.014
Every world receives help from all seven Master Spirits, but it receives the coloring of its individual character only from the one Master Spirit in charge of its superuniverse. So a being born in a superuniverse, whether human or angel, carries the traits of that Master Spirit forever. This mark is never fully erased, even after the long training of Havona and Paradise. The Master Spirit does not touch our minds directly, but the Creative Spirit of the local universe, who cares for us, is very much like the Master Spirit of Orvonton.
## p16.015
6. The Cosmic Mind
## p16.016
The Seven Master Spirits are the source of the cosmic mind. The basis of the minds with which we think and understand also comes, in the end, from them. The cosmic mind responds to three kinds of reality. The first is the scientific eye that recognizes cause and effect; the second is the sense of duty that feels right and wrong; and the third is the spiritual insight that worships God. These three are inborn gifts for everyone, but not many people have the courage to develop them.
## p16.017
When these three come together as one within a person, they make a strong character in which science, morality, and true faith go together. Developing these inborn abilities is the purpose of education, and tying them together into one is the purpose of personality.
## p16.018
7. Morals, Virtue, and Personality
## p16.019
What most sets human beings apart from animals is their moral and religious nature. Animals usually learn only by trying things out directly, but a person “⟦Q 16:7.3⟧can look before it leaps⟦/Q⟧”. People can ask not only about methods but also whether the goal itself is worth reaching. Virtue is not memorizing the names of good things; it is living out the choice of good over evil every day.
## p16.020
Morality cannot be forced to grow by law or by power. It spreads, as if by contagion, when people meet someone whose life has a moral fragrance. So the highest virtue is to choose, with all your heart, to do the will of the Father in heaven.
## p16.021
8. Urantia Personality
## p16.022
Personality is a gift from the Universal Father. Personalities can be similar, but they are never exactly the same. From this time on, the long confusion of the universe slowly begins to end. After endless mercy and patience have all been shown, justice and righteousness win. The Master Son is able to “⟦Q 21:5.10⟧pour out upon all flesh⟦/Q⟧” the Spirit of Truth, and so he stays closely connected with the worlds where he lived.
## p21.018
6. Destiny of the Master Michaels
## p21.019
No one can say for certain what the far future of the Master Michaels will be. But wonderful powers not yet revealed are hidden within them, and it is thought that someday, far in the future, they will take up new service in the outer universes. On earth, Jesus said, “⟦Q 21:6.4⟧I am the way, the truth, and the life.⟦/Q⟧”
## p21.020
In eternity the Michaels will literally become the way, the truth, and the life for all beings, lighting up the path to God. The fact that the One who made us lived as a human being just like us means that he knows our joys and our pains better than anyone.
## p22.001
Introduction
## p22.002
Besides the descending Sons and the ascending Sons, there is a third group of the Sons of God: the Trinitized Sons. They are divided into the Deity-trinitized Sons, the Trinity-embraced Sons, and the creature-trinitized sons. The Deity-trinitized Sons have not yet been revealed, so this paper talks about the other two groups, especially the Trinity-embraced Sons.
## p22.003
1. The Trinity-Embraced Sons
## p22.004
The Trinity-embraced Sons are beings who were first born in some other way but were later embraced by the Paradise Trinity and given the work of the Trinity. There are seven orders: the Mighty Messengers, Those High in Authority, Those without Name and Number, the Trinitized Custodians, the Trinitized Ambassadors, the Celestial Guardians, and the High Son Assistants. The first three orders are mortals who became one with their Thought Adjusters and rose all the way to Paradise.
## p22.005
All of them help the Ancients of Days in the superuniverses. Their number keeps growing, and so far not a single one of them has ever gone astray.
## p22.006
2. The Mighty Messengers
## p22.007
The Mighty Messengers are mortals who, on their path of ascension, stayed loyal without wavering when those over them rebelled. Those who managed to stop a rebellion before it could happen also become Mighty Messengers. The one who wrote this paper is a Mighty Messenger too. He says that he and a companion from his mortal days were separated many times, but in the end they were embraced by the Trinity together in the same group, and even now the two of them often go out on assignments together.
## p22.008
The Mighty Messengers defend individuals and planets in the superuniverse courts, and they stay on many worlds as observers and advisers. Because they remember the whole path of ascension they have traveled, they deeply understand creatures of time like us. Once we have left the body behind, we will be able to talk freely with them.
## p22.009
3. Those High in Authority
## p22.010
Those High in Authority are mortals who showed outstanding skill in administration during their long ascension. In the superuniverse courts, they act as prosecutors who stand up for justice. But since these prosecutors once walked the same road we do, even if we end up standing in court because of a mistake, there is almost no chance we will be treated unfairly.
## p22.011
4. Those Without Name and Number
## p22.012
Those without Name and Number are mortals who developed a greater power of worship than anyone else. Translated more exactly, their name would be “⟦Q 22:4.1⟧Those above Name and Number⟦/Q⟧”. Because they have the most spiritually gifted minds, they serve as the highest jury in the superuniverse.
## p22.013
Being chosen for these three orders is not decided by anyone at will; it comes about naturally according to each one's experience and spiritual worth. We who read this too may someday rise to Paradise, be embraced by the Trinity, and, like the writer of this paper, be given the task of bringing truth to some planet.
## p22.014
5. The Trinitized Custodians
## p22.015
The Trinitized Custodians are seraphic angels and midwayers who have ascended and reached Paradise. They earn this honor because they bravely stayed with the mortals they cared for all the way to the end. The guardian angel who watched over the writer of this paper also ascended with him and now works as a custodian on Uversa. The custodians look after records, plans, and many kinds of projects.
## p22.016
6. The Trinitized Ambassadors
## p22.017
Not every ascending mortal becomes one with a Thought Adjuster. Some become one with the Spirit, and some with the Son. The Trinitized Ambassadors are chosen from among the mortals who reached Paradise after becoming one with the Spirit or the Son in this way. As special envoys of the Ancients of Days, they take on countless tasks all over the superuniverse.
## p22.018
7. Technique of Trinitization
## p22.019
Trinitization is the highest creative act that perfected spirit beings can perform. Paradise-Havona personalities and some finaliters can pair up, take hold together of one truth of the universe that no one has yet made real, and through long ages of effort bring it to birth as a new being. This takes an almost unbelievable length of time, and it often fails even when no one has done anything wrong.
## p22.020
When it succeeds, the two parents who brought forth the new son become spiritually like one, so that wherever one goes, the other goes too. The sons born this way include the ascender-trinitized sons, the Paradise-Havona-trinitized sons, and the Trinitized Sons of Destiny, who are being prepared for the far future.
## p22.021
8. The Creature-Trinitized Sons
## p22.022
Creature-trinitized sons, too, all receive their personality from the Universal Father. At first they work on the spirit spheres of Paradise, and then they are adopted by the Trinity Teacher Sons and learn more in the local universes. When the Teacher Sons recommend them, they are embraced by the Trinity again and become “⟦Q 22:8.4⟧twice-trinitized⟦/Q⟧” beings. Those who are not take up many other kinds of work, such as becoming companions of the Master Spirits or the Mother Spirits.
## p22.023
9. The Celestial Guardians
## p22.024
The Celestial Guardians are one kind of twice-trinitized sons. They serve as officers of the superuniverse courts and deliver summonses and verdicts. They are wonderful beings, but they do not have the deep experience of ascenders who really climbed up from the dark worlds. So they look at the ascension experience of mortals like us with envious eyes.
## p22.025
The writer says, “⟦Q 22:9.7⟧There is simply nothing in all universal existence which can take the place of actual personal experience⟦/Q⟧”. Our path of starting from a low place and growing one step at a time is not something to be ashamed of; it is a privilege to be thankful for.
## p22.026
10. High Son Assistants
## p22.027
The High Son Assistants are like secretaries who help the high Sons of the superuniverse. Their greatest strength is that each one carries one great idea as a person. So when a problem needs solving, having a High Son Assistant who carries exactly the right idea for that problem is a help beyond measure.
## p22.028
They are kind, loyal, wise, and very humble. They understand their own one idea better than anyone, but they eagerly try to learn other things even from ascending mortals. In this way, all the Trinitized Sons are wisely helping us move forward toward Havona and Paradise.
## p23.001
Introduction
## p23.002
The Solitary Messengers are spirit beings that the Infinite Spirit (the third person of God) made all by himself. They are the first and oldest order among the high personalities who come from the Infinite Spirit. The Father and the Son did not take part directly in making these beings; it was the first creative act the Infinite Spirit did alone.
## p23.003
The Solitary Messengers all came into being at once in a single act of creation, so their number is fixed. The being who wrote this paper says that he is working with a Solitary Messenger right now. In our superuniverse of Orvonton alone (the one we belong to, among seven huge groups of universes), almost 7,690 trillion of them are at work, and even this is said to be less than one seventh of them all.
## p23.004
1. Nature and Origin of Solitary Messengers
## p23.005
The Infinite Spirit made the Solitary Messengers right after he made the Seven Spirits of Havona (the perfect central universe around Paradise). So apart from Paradise and Havona, nothing existed before them. Though they have been around since very long ago, they all remember when they began, and they are the first beings made by the Infinite Spirit who are aware of time. All of them were made fully grown from the start and are equal to one another; they differ only in the work they are given. Humans start out on faraway worlds and climb toward the center, but the Solitary Messengers start at the center and long to go out to the farthest worlds.
## p23.006
Their name is Solitary Messengers, but they are not lonely beings. They really do like to work alone, and they are the only beings in all creation who can enjoy being by themselves. They can also hear every broadcast where they are, so they are always connected with the news of the universe. They are so loyal that not a single Solitary Messenger has ever been recorded as falling into darkness and rebelling.
## p23.007
There is also a reason why they must travel alone. When two or more of them come close together, their connection with the power circuits coming from Paradise is cut off; they are “⟦Q 23:1.9⟧short circuited⟦/Q⟧”, so to speak. So when they get near each other, a warning goes off by itself and they move apart. They cannot have children or multiply themselves, but they help in almost every field and save the universe administrators a great deal of time.
## p23.008
2. Assignments of Solitary Messengers
## p23.009
The Solitary Messengers are not tied forever to any one being. They take assignments as they come and follow the direction of whoever is in charge in that region. They have no organization or government among themselves. The Infinite Spirit sends them out on seven kinds of work: messengers of the Paradise Trinity, messengers of the Havona circuits, messengers of the superuniverses, messengers of the local universes, explorers without orders, ambassadors and envoys on special missions, and revealers of truth. They move freely from one kind of work to another.
## p23.010
The messengers of the Trinity have been trusted with the secrets of the Gods and have never once given them away. Here the paper also talks about the suffering on Urantia. The Creators have the power to make this world a paradise, but if they did, strong and noble character could not grow. Our trials and sorrows, too, are part of the divine plan. The messengers of the superuniverses are the only channel through which the rulers of one superuniverse can exchange news directly with the rulers of another. The messenger who works with the writer of this paper once left to gather information and came back almost a million years later.
## p23.011
The explorers roam far-off space that no one knows and find worlds and universes that are just coming into being. They are so sensitive to gravity that they can notice even a small planet that is suitable for life. The ambassadors travel quickly between faraway universes and represent them to one another, and the revealers of truth consider making truth known to the worlds to be their most precious task.
## p23.012
3. Time and Space Services of Solitary Messengers
## p23.013
In the universe there are spirits that can go anywhere in an instant, but they have no personality. On the other hand, personal beings like angels and humans are bound by time and space. Some beings move at the speed of light, and the seraphim (one order of angels) fly about three times as fast as that. But in between, the only beings who move incredibly fast and yet have personality are the Solitary Messengers. The companion of the writer of this paper is said to have traveled at more than eight hundred billion miles per second.
## p23.014
The Solitary Messengers have no form, but they clearly have personality. Usually, the freer a being is from time and space, the fewer privileges of personality it has, but the Solitary Messengers are the exception. That is why it is said that among the countless beings who help with the work of the superuniverses, none are as useful as they are. However big the universe grows, though, no more Solitary Messengers will be made, and the growing work will be taken up by other beings who appear in the new creations.
## p23.015
4. Special Ministry of Solitary Messengers
## p23.016
The Solitary Messengers are co-ordinators who connect all the different kinds of spirit beings. Thanks to them, beings of completely different kinds can understand each other and get along like friends. Also, when a finaliter (a human who has finished the ascension) and a Paradise Citizen bring a new being into existence together, that being is sent to a mysterious world called Vicegerington, and a Solitary Messenger always goes along as its guardian and companion. The two never leave that place again.
## p23.017
The number of Solitary Messengers is fixed, but this kind of thing can keep happening without end, so someday there may not be enough Solitary Messengers. Even the celestial beings do not know who will take over their work then. This paper says this is a sign that the universe is being newly prepared for growth in the far outer space, and it urges us to look forward to the future, as they do, “⟦Q 23:4.6⟧with keen relish and ever-heightening expectation⟦/Q⟧”. Looking toward tomorrow with excitement and without fear, even when there is much we do not know, is a precious lesson for us too.
## p24.001
Introduction
## p24.002
The beings who come from the Infinite Spirit (the third person of God) fall into three large groups: the higher personalities, the messengers of space, and the ministering spirits who teach and help those who are climbing upward. This paper introduces the first group, the higher personalities. There are seven orders of them: the Solitary Messengers, the Universe Circuit Supervisors, the Census Directors, the Personal Aids of the Infinite Spirit, the Associate Inspectors, the Assigned Sentinels, and the Graduate Guides.
## p24.003
Each of them lives in a different place. The Solitary Messengers have no fixed headquarters and travel the whole universe, the Personal Aids live on Paradise, and the Graduate Guides live in Havona (the perfect central universe around Paradise). The Solitary Messengers were already covered in the last paper, so here we look at the other six orders.
## p24.004
1. The Universe Circuit Supervisors
## p24.005
The flow of universe power and spiritual energy may seem to move all by itself, but in fact someone is wisely taking care of it. Material energy is looked after by the power directors, and the circuits of spirit energy that support spirit beings and morontia beings (the stage between material and spiritual life) are looked after by the Universe Circuit Supervisors. They are divided into four orders and placed in Havona, the superuniverses, and the local universes.
## p24.006
The circuit supervisors work forever in the post they are first given, so they know the problems of that place very deeply. On Salvington, the capital of our local universe, there is a supervisor who has worked there since the beginning. When the leader of a planet rebels against God, they are the ones who cut that world off from the higher spiritual circuits and isolate it. They are true personalities, but they have a special kind of personality that no other beings have. On our way to Paradise we will get to know them, but we will not become close friends, because they give their whole hearts only to their work.
## p24.007
2. The Census Directors
## p24.008
The Census Directors count all the will creatures of the universe, that is, all the beings who can make their own choices. The moment a being uses its will for the first time, they notice right away and register it. So at any time they can tell how many will beings there are and where. But they are not needed on Paradise, where the Deities already know everything.
## p24.009
The Census Director in charge of our local universe knows each one of us living on Urantia (our earth) right now, and he records it the moment we finish our lives. But they are not recorders who write down what we have done; they count only the fact that a will exists. They are never lazy, and they never make mistakes.
## p24.010
3. Personal Aids of the Infinite Spirit
## p24.011
Not much is known about when or how the Personal Aids of the Infinite Spirit were made, and their number is thought to be far more than trillions. They live on Paradise and work only for the Infinite Spirit, and when they are given a command, they flash like lightning to the very edges of creation and back. They are all alike and equal, and they are so unseen that other spirit beings cannot sense their presence. That makes them the only beings we will almost never meet on our way up to Paradise.
## p24.012
4. The Associate Inspectors
## p24.013
The Seven Supreme Executives who live on Paradise are like a committee that cares for the seven superuniverses together. The Associate Inspectors are high observers who represent the authority of these Supreme Executives in the local universes. Seven hundred thousand of them were made very long ago, and one stays at the capital of each local universe. They receive reports from the Assigned Sentinels below them and pass them straight on to the Supreme Executive of their superuniverse.
## p24.014
5. The Assigned Sentinels
## p24.015
There are exactly seven billion Assigned Sentinels, and they are the liaison representatives who connect the Supreme Executives with each local system. One stays in each system within a local universe, and they usually change posts every thousand years. They do not sit in the councils that directly decide the affairs of the system; they focus on watching everything about that system and reporting it to the Associate Inspector. In this way the Supreme Executives, the Associate Inspectors, and the Assigned Sentinels together form a network that efficiently watches over and co-ordinates the whole universe.
## p24.016
6. The Graduate Guides
## p24.017
The Graduate Guides are teachers who work in Havona. They lead the education and training through the seven circuits of Havona so that those who have come up from the worlds of time and space can get ready to enter Paradise. The guide who greets us when we first arrive on the outer circuit of Havona will stay by our side until we have finished the whole course of Havona and fall into the last sleep before Paradise. This paper says that our understanding now is not meant to stay so narrow forever.
## p24.018
At first there were no Graduate Guides. When the first human pilgrim arrived in Havona, the first Graduate Guide, Malvorian, appeared, welcomed him, and led him step by step all the way to Paradise. The name of that first pilgrim was Grandfanda. When he arrived, the greatest joy of all time spread through heaven, and to announce it, the first broadcast across the whole universe began. If we do not reject God's plan, someday we too will meet these noble guides face to face.
## p24.019
7. Origin of the Graduate Guides
## p24.020
Where did the Graduate Guides come from? The celestial beings believe that they are Havona servitals (beings born in the central universe who serve in the superuniverses) with a great deal of experience who have been changed. When a servital who has come back after finishing many tours of superuniverse service is embraced by God on Paradise and disappears, a new Graduate Guide appears on the outer circuit of Havona a little less than three days later. The number of servitals who have disappeared and the number of guides who have appeared are exactly the same. Also, the guides and the servitals understand and care for each other amazingly deeply.
## p24.021
After Grandfanda arrived, Havona changed greatly to welcome human ascenders. This paper explains that this change started with the Supreme Being, the God of evolving creatures, and that the appearance of the Graduate Guides is one part of it. The fact that so many beings are preparing and waiting for us as we travel such a long road gives us the strong comfort that we are never alone.
