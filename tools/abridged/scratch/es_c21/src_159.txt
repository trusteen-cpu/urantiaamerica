## 159.title   HINT: La Gira por la Decápolis
"The Decapolis Tour"
## 159.b0.ask
"“On a tour of the Decapolis without a single miracle, what did Jesus teach about forgiveness, about the Scriptures, and about overcoming evil with good?”"
## 159.b1.h
"Overview"
## 159.b2.p
"In Paper 158 Jesus came down from the mount of transfiguration, spoke for the first time of his death and resurrection, and taught that the greatest is the one who humbles himself like a little child. Paper 159 follows the four-week tour of the Decapolis that came next. On August 18 Jesus paired each apostle with an evangelist and sent them out in twelve groups, and they labored in Gerasa, Gadara, Philadelphia, and many other cities."
## 159.b3.p
"Throughout this tour no miracles of healing or other extraordinary events occurred. Instead Jesus visited the groups in turn and taught quietly in the evenings, and his teaching ranged widely: forgiveness, the attitude of a teacher, how to regard the Scriptures, and a positive religion."
## 159.b4.p
"What, then, did Jesus most want to impress upon his followers during this month without miracles?"

## 159.b7.h   HINT: 1. El Sermón del Perdón
"1. The Sermon on Forgiveness"
## 159.b8.p
"At Hippos Jesus taught forgiveness through the story of the shepherd who keeps up the search for one lost sheep until he finds it. The Father goes out to find sinners before they have even thought of repentance. Since the Father forgives us, we should learn to forgive one another, and Jesus described the steps to take when a brother does wrong."

## 159.b11.p
"Jesus taught the dangers of sitting in personal judgment on one’s fellows. Because an individual’s verdict may be warped by prejudice or distorted by passion, he invested legislative and judicial authority in the group, not in the individual. He sought always to minimize the elements of unfairness, retaliation, and vengeance."
## 159.b12.h   HINT: 2. El Extraño Predicador
"2. The Strange Preacher"
## 159.b13.p
"At Gamala John reported that he had forbidden a man who taught and cast out devils in Jesus’ name without following them. Jesus told him not to forbid such a man, and to rejoice that their teaching was already spreading beyond their personal influence. In matters of this sort it is better to reckon that he who is not against us is for us; even a cup of cold water given to a thirsty soul is recorded by the Father’s messengers."

## 159.b16.h   HINT: 3. La Instrucción para Maestros y Creyentes
"3. Instruction for Teachers and Believers"
## 159.b17.p
"At Edrei Jesus gave the principles that should guide those who teach the gospel. First of all, they must respect the personality of man. Spiritual victories can be won only by spiritual power; people are not to be crushed by the weight of logic or overawed by shrewd eloquence, nor moved by appeals to fear or mere sentiment."

## 159.b19.p
"Faith is to religion what sails are to a ship: an addition of power, not an added burden. The believer has only one battle, and that is against doubt. To preach the gospel is simply to teach friendship with God, and they were not to portray their teacher as a man of sorrows; this religion throbs with new life, joy, and good humor."
## 159.b21.h   HINT: 4. La Conversación con Natanael
"4. The Talk with Nathaniel"
## 159.b22.p
"At Abila Nathaniel quietly asked what the truth about the Scriptures was. Jesus answered that he did not regard them as the rabbis did, and asked Nathaniel not to pass this on to his brethren, who were not yet prepared for it."

## 159.b24.p
"Jesus told Nathaniel never to believe the records saying that the God of love ordered his forefathers to slay all their enemies, men, women, and children; such records are the words of men, not the word of God. He also explained that they were not writing down the gospel now, lest after his departure they divide into groups contending over its interpretation. The saddest thing of all was that some teachers knew these truths but, as moral cowards, kept them from the people."

## 159.b29.p
"Jesus made the fatherhood of God the cornerstone of his religion, and so the practice of the brotherhood of man became imperative. The worship of God and the service of man became the sum and substance of his religion. He put the spirit of positive action into passive doctrines, and he even opposed purely passive nonresistance."

## 159.b31.p
"Turning the other cheek does not mean standing dumb and passive; it means actively leading the brother in error toward better ways. If forced to carry a stranger’s pack for a mile, one should not grumble under one’s breath but carry it a second mile. Jesus revealed a God who goes in search of the lost sheep, turned the negative golden rule into a positive admonition, and brought the philosophy of religion from heaven down to earth."
## 159.b32.h   HINT: 6. El Retorno a Magadán
"6. The Return to Magadan"
## 159.b33.p
"The four-week mission was moderately successful: hundreds were received into the kingdom, and the apostles and evangelists gained valuable experience in working without Jesus’ immediate presence. On September 16 everyone gathered at Magadan Park to consider future plans."
## 159.b34.p
"In these dark days David’s messengers kept the believers throughout Palestine in touch with one another and with Jesus, and collected funds to support Jesus and the families of the apostles and evangelists. David even kept an overnight relay of runners between Jerusalem and Bethsaida. Now came a week of rest, the last rest before they reached Jerusalem."
## 159.b35.h
"Conclusion"
## 159.b36.p
"Paper 159 shows how rich in teaching a month without miracles could be. Jesus taught the forgiveness of a Father who goes out first to find, and told his followers to forgive without limit and to rejoice even in the good done by those outside their circle. He told teachers to respect personality and self-respect and never to frighten people, and he told Nathaniel to treasure the Scriptures as a precious human record while taking the living spirit as the authority of truth. Above all, his religion was not one of passive endurance but a positive religion that actively overcomes evil with good."
