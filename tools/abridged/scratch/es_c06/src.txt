41.b15.p "More than two thousand brilliant suns shine in Satania, and our own sun is an average blazing orb. Of the thirty suns nearest to it, only three are brighter. Yet space is so roomy that these giants have as much elbow room as a dozen oranges would have circulating inside the earth, were it a hollow globe."

41.b18.h "4. Sun Density"
41.b19.p "Our sun is a gas, yet it has about one and one-half times the density of water. How can that be? Gaseous, liquid, and solid states are matters of how atoms and molecules relate to each other, but density is a relationship of space and mass. That is why a cooling star can be gaseous and tremendously dense at the same time."

48.b40.h "Conclusion"
